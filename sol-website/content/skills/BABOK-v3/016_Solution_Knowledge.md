# Solution Knowledge

## Overview

Understanding of the solution domain — the technologies, products, processes, and methodologies relevant to the solutions the BA is specifying.

## Context & Usage

Essential when evaluating design options (build vs. buy vs. modify), specifying non-functional requirements, performing vendor assessment, and communicating with technical teams. Deep solution knowledge helps the BA distinguish 'what is needed' from 'how to build it' without over-specifying implementation.
