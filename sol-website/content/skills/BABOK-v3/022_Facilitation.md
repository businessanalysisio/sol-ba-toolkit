# Facilitation

## Overview

Guiding groups to productive outcomes without imposing personal views. Facilitation is the primary skill for workshops and collaborative sessions — it keeps groups focused, inclusive, and moving toward decisions.

## Context & Usage

Applied when running workshops, collaborative games, focus groups, and any group decision-making session. Key practices: set clear objectives, design the agenda, manage participation (draw out quiet voices, contain dominant ones), capture decisions visibly, and confirm next steps. The facilitator is neutral — they own the process, not the content.
