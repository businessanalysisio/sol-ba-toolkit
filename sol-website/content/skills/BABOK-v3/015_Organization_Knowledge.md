# Organization Knowledge

## Overview

Knowledge of how the specific organization works — its culture, power structures, informal networks, policies, and political landscape.

## Context & Usage

Applied in stakeholder engagement (knowing who influences decisions beyond formal titles), communication planning (tailoring message to organizational culture), and governance design (understanding who must approve what). Learn through stakeholder analysis, observation, and background research.
