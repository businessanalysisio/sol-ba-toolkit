# Meeting facilitation (running productive meetings/workshops toward a clear outcome)

## Overview

Meeting facilitation (running productive meetings/workshops toward a clear outcome) is a business analysis practice for handling stakeholder collaboration and elicitation. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when meeting facilitation is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
