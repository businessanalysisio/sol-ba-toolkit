# Identifying transformational enterprise initiatives

## Overview

Identifying transformational enterprise initiatives is a business analysis practice for handling business analysis and decision making. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when identifying transformational enterprise initiatives is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
