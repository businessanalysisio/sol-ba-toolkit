# Tenacity (persistence through ambiguity, chasing sign-offs, holding focus in workshops)

## Overview

Tenacity (persistence through ambiguity, chasing sign-offs, holding focus in workshops) is a business analysis practice for handling stakeholder collaboration and elicitation. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when tenacity is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
