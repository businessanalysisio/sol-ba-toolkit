# Preparing an organization for business agility

## Overview

Preparing an organization for business agility is a business analysis practice for handling business analysis and decision making. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when preparing an organization for business agility is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
