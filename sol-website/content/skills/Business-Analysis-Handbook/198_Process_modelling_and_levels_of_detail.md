# Process modelling and levels of detail

## Overview

Process modelling and levels of detail is a business analysis practice for handling process analysis and operational improvement. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when process modelling and levels of detail is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
