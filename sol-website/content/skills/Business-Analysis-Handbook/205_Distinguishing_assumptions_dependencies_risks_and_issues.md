# Distinguishing assumptions, dependencies, risks, and issues

## Overview

Distinguishing assumptions, dependencies, risks, and issues is a business analysis practice for handling risk, change, and governance. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when distinguishing assumptions, dependencies, risks, and issues is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
