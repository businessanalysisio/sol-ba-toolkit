# Real Options

## Overview

Real Options is a product ownership analysis technique for improving value delivery. It helps teams connect customer needs, product decisions, backlog items, and delivery outcomes.

## Context & Usage

Use this in discovery, backlog refinement, roadmap planning, or sprint preparation. Connect the technique to customer value, prioritization, acceptance criteria, and measurable outcomes.
