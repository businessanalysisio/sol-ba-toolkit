# Boston Box / BCG matrix

## Overview

The Boston Consulting Group portfolio matrix, which positions an organisation's products, services or business units on two axes — relative market share and market growth rate — yielding four categories: Stars (high share, high growth), Cash Cows (high share, low growth), Question Marks or Wildcats (low share, high growth) and Dogs (low share, low growth). It guides investment, hold, harvest and divest decisions across the portfolio.

## Context & Usage

Applied in strategy analysis to review the balance of a product or service portfolio and to understand where cash is generated and where it should be invested. A BA uses it to frame discussions about which offerings a change initiative should support, grow or retire, and as internal analysis input to SWOT. Tip: market share and growth data can be hard to establish precisely — treat placements as indicative and validate with stakeholders rather than presenting them as exact.
