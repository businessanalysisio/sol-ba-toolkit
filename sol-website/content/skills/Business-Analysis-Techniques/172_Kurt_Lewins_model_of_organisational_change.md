# Kurt Lewin's model of organisational change

## Overview

Kurt Lewin's model of organisational change is a business analysis technique for risk, change, and governance. It provides a structured way to examine the situation, compare options, and produce an artifact that stakeholders can review.

## Context & Usage

Use this technique during risk, change, and governance work when a team needs a shared view of facts, options, impacts, or decisions. Define scope first, facilitate input from relevant stakeholders, and record outcomes with owners and follow-up actions.
