# Ansoff's matrix / Ansoff's Box

## Overview

Igor Ansoff's product–market growth matrix, which classifies growth strategies along two axes — existing versus new products, and existing versus new markets — producing four options: market penetration (existing/existing), market development (existing product, new market), product development (new product, existing market) and diversification (new/new). Risk increases as the organisation moves away from what it knows, with diversification the riskiest quadrant.

## Context & Usage

Used during strategy analysis and options identification to structure discussion about how an organisation intends to grow and how much risk each route carries. A BA applies it when evaluating strategic options or testing that a proposed project fits the organisation's growth strategy. Tip: pair each quadrant under consideration with the resource audit findings — a growth option is only viable if the organisation has, or can acquire, the resources it demands.
