# MOST / VMOST analysis

## Overview

An internal environment analysis technique that examines an organisation's Mission, Objectives, Strategy and Tactics (VMOST adds Vision at the top). It tests whether these four layers exist, are clearly articulated, and align with one another — from the enduring purpose (mission) through measurable objectives to the strategy and the detailed tactics that deliver it.

## Context & Usage

Applied during strategy analysis or at project initiation to check that a proposed change supports the organisation's declared direction, and to expose misalignment (for example, tactics that contradict the stated strategy). A BA also uses MOST to derive evaluation criteria for options and business cases. Tip: objectives should be SMART; if you cannot find documented objectives, eliciting and confirming them is itself a valuable outcome of the analysis.
