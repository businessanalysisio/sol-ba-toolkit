# SWOT analysis

## Overview

A summarising framework that consolidates strategic analysis into four quadrants: Strengths and Weaknesses (internal, within the organisation's control) and Opportunities and Threats (external, arising from the business environment). SWOT is not a primary analysis technique in itself — it structures and presents the findings of techniques such as PESTLE, Five Forces, MOST and Resource Audit.

## Context & Usage

Used at the strategy analysis stage, and at the start of programmes and business cases, to give decision-makers a one-page view of the organisation's strategic position. A BA should populate it from evidence produced by prior internal and external analysis rather than brainstorming it cold, and should keep entries specific and supportable. Tip: follow up with TOWS to turn the quadrants into actionable strategic options rather than leaving SWOT as a static list.
