# PESTLE / PEST / PESTEL / PESTLIED / STEEPLE analysis

## Overview

A framework for analysing the external business environment by examining Political, Economic, Socio-cultural, Technological, Legal and Environmental factors that may affect an organisation. Variants adjust the categories: PEST drops Legal/Environmental, PESTLIED adds International and Demographic factors, and STEEPLE adds Ethics. The output is a structured list of external forces, each assessed for its potential impact and likelihood.

## Context & Usage

Applied during strategy analysis, at the outset of a change initiative or business case, to identify external opportunities and threats beyond the organisation's control. A BA typically runs PESTLE in a workshop, then feeds the significant factors into the opportunities and threats quadrants of a SWOT. Tip: focus on factors that genuinely affect the organisation's industry and time horizon rather than producing an exhaustive generic list, and revisit the analysis periodically as the environment changes.
