# Probable Purchaser

## Overview

Probable Purchaser is a business mental model for understanding business analysis and decision making. It gives operators a compact lens for diagnosing a situation, naming the forces at work, and choosing a practical next action without overcomplicating the analysis.

## Context & Usage

Use this model when a business conversation needs clearer thinking about business analysis and decision making. Start by naming the current situation, identify which assumptions or constraints are driving outcomes, then use the model to choose one experiment, policy, metric, or decision to test next.
