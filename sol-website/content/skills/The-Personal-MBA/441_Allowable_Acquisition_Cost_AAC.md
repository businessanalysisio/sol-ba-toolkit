# Allowable Acquisition Cost (AAC)

## Overview

Allowable Acquisition Cost (AAC) is a business mental model for understanding commercial and financial analysis. It gives operators a compact lens for diagnosing a situation, naming the forces at work, and choosing a practical next action without overcomplicating the analysis.

## Context & Usage

Use this model when a business conversation needs clearer thinking about commercial and financial analysis. Start by naming the current situation, identify which assumptions or constraints are driving outcomes, then use the model to choose one experiment, policy, metric, or decision to test next.
