# Adapting communication to different stakeholder roles (executives, product owners, sponsors, PMs, SMEs, QA, UX, Scrum Masters, architects, developers, testers, trainers, vendors, customers)

## Overview

Adapting communication to different stakeholder roles (executives, product owners, sponsors, PMs, SMEs, QA, UX, Scrum Masters, architects, developers, testers, trainers, vendors, customers) is a practical BA capability for progressing from unclear business need to agreed requirements and delivery-ready understanding.

## Context & Usage

Apply this when entering a new initiative, preparing elicitation, analyzing findings, or driving agreement. The expected output should be specific enough to support stakeholder review and next-step planning.
