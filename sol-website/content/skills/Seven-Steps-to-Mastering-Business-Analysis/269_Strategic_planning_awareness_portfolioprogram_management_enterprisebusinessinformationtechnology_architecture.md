# Strategic planning awareness (portfolio/program management, enterprise/business/information/technology architecture)

## Overview

Strategic planning awareness (portfolio/program management, enterprise/business/information/technology architecture) is a practical BA capability for progressing from unclear business need to agreed requirements and delivery-ready understanding.

## Context & Usage

Apply this when entering a new initiative, preparing elicitation, analyzing findings, or driving agreement. The expected output should be specific enough to support stakeholder review and next-step planning.
