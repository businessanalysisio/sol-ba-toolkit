# Protecting Your Business (legal/risk)

## Overview

Protecting Your Business (legal/risk) covers a core founder activity in risk, change, and governance. It helps turn an early business idea into clear operating choices, visible trade-offs, and actions that can be tested with customers, partners, regulators, or the market.

## Context & Usage

Apply this during venture discovery, launch planning, or operating review. Capture the decision to be made, the evidence available, the risks of acting too early or too late, and the next customer-facing or operational step.
