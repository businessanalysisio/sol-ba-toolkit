# Outcome Frame / Outcome orientation / Outcome thinking

## Overview

Outcome Frame / Outcome orientation / Outcome thinking is a business analysis technique for business analysis and decision making. It provides a structured way to examine the situation, compare options, and produce an artifact that stakeholders can review.

## Context & Usage

Use this technique during business analysis and decision making work when a team needs a shared view of facts, options, impacts, or decisions. Define scope first, facilitate input from relevant stakeholders, and record outcomes with owners and follow-up actions.
