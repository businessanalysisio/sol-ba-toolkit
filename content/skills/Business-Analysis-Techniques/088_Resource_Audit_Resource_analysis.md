# Resource Audit / Resource analysis

## Overview

An internal analysis technique that inventories and evaluates an organisation's resources across five categories: physical (buildings, plant, equipment), financial (funds, credit, investor confidence), human (staff numbers, skills, adaptability), know-how (intellectual property, methods, knowledge) and reputation (brand, goodwill, standing with customers and regulators). Each category is assessed for adequacy relative to the organisation's strategy.

## Context & Usage

Used in strategy analysis to identify internal strengths and weaknesses, which feed directly into the corresponding quadrants of a SWOT. A BA applies it when assessing whether the organisation has the capacity and capability to pursue a strategic option or deliver a proposed change. Tip: assess resources against what the strategy demands, not in the abstract — a resource is only a strength if it supports the intended direction.
