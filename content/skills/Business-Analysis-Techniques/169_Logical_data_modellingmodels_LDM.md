# Logical data modelling/models (LDM)

## Overview

Logical data modelling/models (LDM) is a business analysis technique for analytics, evidence, and decision support. It provides a structured way to examine the situation, compare options, and produce an artifact that stakeholders can review.

## Context & Usage

Use this technique during analytics, evidence, and decision support work when a team needs a shared view of facts, options, impacts, or decisions. Define scope first, facilitate input from relevant stakeholders, and record outcomes with owners and follow-up actions.
