# Interviewing

## Overview

Interviewing is a business analysis technique for stakeholder collaboration and elicitation. It provides a structured way to examine the situation, compare options, and produce an artifact that stakeholders can review.

## Context & Usage

Use this technique during stakeholder collaboration and elicitation work when a team needs a shared view of facts, options, impacts, or decisions. Define scope first, facilitate input from relevant stakeholders, and record outcomes with owners and follow-up actions.
