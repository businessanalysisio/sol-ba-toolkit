# Identifying solution constraints

## Overview

Identifying solution constraints is a business analysis practice for handling business analysis and decision making. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when identifying solution constraints is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
