# Problem solving (identifying, communicating, and proposing resolutions to complex issues)

## Overview

Problem solving (identifying, communicating, and proposing resolutions to complex issues) is a business analysis practice for handling risk, change, and governance. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when problem solving is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
