# Requirements hierarchy and structuring

## Overview

Requirements hierarchy and structuring is a business analysis practice for handling requirements and product definition. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when requirements hierarchy and structuring is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
