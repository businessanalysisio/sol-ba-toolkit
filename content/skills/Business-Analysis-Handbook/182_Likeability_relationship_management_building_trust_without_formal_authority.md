# Likeability / relationship management (building trust without formal authority)

## Overview

Likeability / relationship management (building trust without formal authority) is a business analysis practice for handling team effectiveness and organizational capability. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when likeability / relationship management is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
