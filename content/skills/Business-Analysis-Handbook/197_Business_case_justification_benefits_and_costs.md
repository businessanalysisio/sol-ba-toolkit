# Business case justification (benefits and costs)

## Overview

Business case justification (benefits and costs) is a business analysis practice for handling commercial and financial analysis. It helps the BA move from ambiguous stakeholder input to structured analysis, documented decisions, and actionable outputs.

## Context & Usage

Use this when business case justification is creating uncertainty, disagreement, or missing detail. The BA should clarify purpose, collect evidence, involve the right stakeholders, and convert the result into a documented artifact or decision.
