# The Iron Law of the Market

## Overview

The Iron Law of the Market is a business mental model for understanding strategy, market fit, and growth. It gives operators a compact lens for diagnosing a situation, naming the forces at work, and choosing a practical next action without overcomplicating the analysis.

## Context & Usage

Use this model when a business conversation needs clearer thinking about strategy, market fit, and growth. Start by naming the current situation, identify which assumptions or constraints are driving outcomes, then use the model to choose one experiment, policy, metric, or decision to test next.
