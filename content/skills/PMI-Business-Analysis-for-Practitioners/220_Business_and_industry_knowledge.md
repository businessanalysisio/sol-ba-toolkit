# Business and industry knowledge

## Overview

Business and industry knowledge describes a practitioner capability used to support project and product outcomes. It helps the BA align stakeholders, requirements, delivery constraints, and value measures.

## Context & Usage

Use this capability across initiation, planning, execution, and evaluation when business and industry knowledge affects scope, stakeholder alignment, quality, or benefit realization.

## Related Techniques / Tools

* Needs Assessment, Traceability, Solution Evaluation
