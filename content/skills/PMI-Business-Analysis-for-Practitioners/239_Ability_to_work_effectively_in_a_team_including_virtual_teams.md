# Ability to work effectively in a team (including virtual teams)

## Overview

Ability to work effectively in a team (including virtual teams) describes a practitioner capability used to support project and product outcomes. It helps the BA align stakeholders, requirements, delivery constraints, and value measures.

## Context & Usage

Use this capability across initiation, planning, execution, and evaluation when ability to work effectively in a team affects scope, stakeholder alignment, quality, or benefit realization.
