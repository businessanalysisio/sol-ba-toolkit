# Probing/questioning discipline (asking good, detailed follow-up questions)

## Overview

Probing/questioning discipline (asking good, detailed follow-up questions) is a practical BA capability for progressing from unclear business need to agreed requirements and delivery-ready understanding.

## Context & Usage

Apply this when entering a new initiative, preparing elicitation, analyzing findings, or driving agreement. The expected output should be specific enough to support stakeholder review and next-step planning.
