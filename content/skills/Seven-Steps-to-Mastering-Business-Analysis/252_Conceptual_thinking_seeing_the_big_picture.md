# Conceptual thinking (seeing the big picture)

## Overview

Conceptual thinking (seeing the big picture) is a practical BA capability for progressing from unclear business need to agreed requirements and delivery-ready understanding.

## Context & Usage

Apply this when entering a new initiative, preparing elicitation, analyzing findings, or driving agreement. The expected output should be specific enough to support stakeholder review and next-step planning.

* Elicitation Planning, Stakeholder Analysis, Requirements Analysis
