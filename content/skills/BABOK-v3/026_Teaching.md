# Teaching

## Overview

Transferring knowledge to stakeholders and team members — enabling them to contribute more effectively to BA activities and to understand BA outputs.

## Context & Usage

Applied when training stakeholders on how to write user stories, when helping product owners refine their backlog, when teaching SMEs what level of detail requirements need, and when mentoring junior BAs. Teaching multiplies the BA's effectiveness by making others better contributors.
