# Interviews

## Overview

Structured or unstructured one-on-one conversations with stakeholders. Interviews are the most flexible elicitation technique — adaptable to any stakeholder, topic, or context.

## Context & Usage

Applied during Elicitation as the primary technique for individual stakeholder engagement. Types: structured (predefined questions asked in order), semi-structured (topic guide with flexible follow-up), and unstructured (open conversation). Best practice: prepare questions (open-ended first, then probing), listen more than speak, paraphrase to confirm, and document immediately after.
