# Personal Accountability

## Overview

Taking ownership of commitments, deliverables, and outcomes. Personal accountability means following through on promises and taking responsibility for results — including mistakes.

## Context & Usage

Demonstrated through reliable delivery of BA work products, honest status reporting, proactive communication of delays or issues, and continuous self-improvement. Builds stakeholder confidence that BA outputs are dependable.
