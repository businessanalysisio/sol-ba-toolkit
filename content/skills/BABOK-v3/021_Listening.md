# Listening

## Overview

Active listening — hearing not just words but intent, concerns, and what is left unsaid. Paraphrasing to confirm understanding is the core practice.

## Context & Usage

Applied in every elicitation activity. In interviews, listening guides probing questions. In workshops, listening detects emerging consensus or hidden disagreement. In stakeholder management, listening uncovers resistance before it becomes a blocker. Most underrated BA skill.
