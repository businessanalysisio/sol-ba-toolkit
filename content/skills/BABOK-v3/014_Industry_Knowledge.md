# Industry Knowledge

## Overview

Domain-specific knowledge — the terminology, regulations, processes, competitive dynamics, and standards of the industry in which the BA operates.

## Context & Usage

Required when entering a new industry domain. Build through document analysis (industry reports, regulatory docs), stakeholder interviews, and observation. Industry knowledge enables the BA to ask informed questions, understand domain constraints, and communicate credibly with subject matter experts.
