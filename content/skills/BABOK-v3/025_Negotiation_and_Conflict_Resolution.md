# Negotiation and Conflict Resolution

## Overview

Finding mutually acceptable solutions to conflicting stakeholder needs. Negotiation and conflict resolution turn disagreement into productive compromise without damaging relationships.

## Context & Usage

Applied when stakeholders have competing priorities (budget vs. scope, quality vs. speed), when requirements conflict, when change requests require trade-off decisions, and during governance escalation. Approach conflicts by understanding each party's underlying interests (not positions) and seeking options that address both.
