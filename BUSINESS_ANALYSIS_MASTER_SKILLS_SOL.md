# Business Analysis & Business Skills — Master Reference

Compiled from nine business analysis and business-fundamentals references:
BABOK Guide v3, Business Analysis Techniques (99 Essential Tools), The
Business Analysis Handbook, PMI's Business Analysis for Practitioners,
Seven Steps to Mastering Business Analysis, the IIBA Guide to Product
Ownership Analysis, Introduction to Business Data Analytics: A
Practitioner View, The Personal MBA, and How to Start Your Own Business.

This is an index of named skills, competencies, and techniques — not a
substitute for the source texts, which explain how and when to apply each
one.
