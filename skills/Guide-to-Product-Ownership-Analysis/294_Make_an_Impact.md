# Make an Impact

## Overview

This POA domain evaluates a product through two integrated lenses that must receive equal attention: Delight Customers and Advance Strategy. Delighting customers rests on three pillars the Guide defines precisely — Target Customers (target only those who will get real value, using personas that explore pain points, feelings, and desires), Product-Market Fit ("that pivotal moment when a product is in the right market and can satisfy and create an emotional connection with the customer"), and Value Proposition (the promise of value that solves customer problems). Advancing strategy covers defining product strategy, integrating across the initiative, and cultivating organizational support. The Guide illustrates the lens with Amazon: its impact is not the drive to deliver the highest-quality products but the obsession with the highest-quality customer experience — prioritizing cost-effective speed of delivery and letting customer ratings speak for product quality.

## Context & Usage

Invoked at the strategy and initiative horizons to keep the product both loved and strategically justified. POA Practitioners in this domain repeatedly ask "Who is impacted or influenced by the product?" and "How will this help this customer?", using the answers to influence the team, prioritize work, and refine planning. It is the counterweight to me-too products: emotionally connected customers have higher lifetime value than merely satisfied ones, so customer delight cannot be an afterthought — but neither can brand fit and delivery against the organization's strategy.
