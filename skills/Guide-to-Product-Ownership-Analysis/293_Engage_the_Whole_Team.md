# Engage the Whole Team

## Overview

A POA domain that redefines "the team": not just the delivery team, but the whole product team — delivery team (BA professional, developer, tester, UX, other specialists), customers or customer representatives, impacted stakeholders, and supporting stakeholders — held collectively responsible for product success. Its engine is the superordinate (shared) goal, "something big enough and compelling enough to aid individuals and groups to overlook personal differences." Three concepts anchor the domain: Shared Vision — with the Guide's template, "For [target customer] who [statement of need or opportunity], the [product name] is a [product category] that [benefit or unique selling point and differentiator]" — Collective Responsibility, and Product Alignment, complemented by an Engage & Empower sub-area covering psychological safety and stakeholder analysis and engagement.

## Context & Usage

Reach for this domain whenever product work is treated as "the delivery team's job" and customers or key stakeholders drift to the sidelines. The Product Owner remains accountable for maximizing value, but the product cannot be built without contributions from the whole team, so the POA Practitioner plans distinct communication and engagement for each group while operating them as a unified team. The domain also carries the agile mindset into POA — respect, courage, collaboration, continuous learning, customer focus, and value maximization — with the practitioner living that mindset visibly rather than merely prescribing it.
