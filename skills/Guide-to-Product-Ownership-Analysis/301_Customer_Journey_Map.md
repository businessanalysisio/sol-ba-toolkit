# Customer Journey Map

## Overview

A visual representation of the end-to-end customer experience, from the customer's perspective, across every touchpoint with a brand and its products. The Guide's canonical stage model runs Awareness → Consideration → Decision → Retention → Advocacy, and its common map components are stages/touchpoints, activities or actions, channels, expectations (what the organization believes each stage should feel like), experiences (the actual outcome from the customer's view), and feelings. Journey maps expose gaps between the desired and received customer experience, identify make-or-break touchpoints, and reveal opportunities to relieve pain points and reduce fragmentation across channels.

## Context & Usage

Created only after articulating a clear objective — understanding or improving current state, designing the future state, building a customer-centric roadmap, or shaping an innovative new offering — because the objective determines the type of journey to map (single- vs. cross-touchpoint, single- vs. cross-channel, front- and backstage). The Guide's eight-step process runs from goal-setting and persona selection through mapping touchpoints, capturing expected value, identifying pain points, prioritizing fixes, and keeping the map updated. Tips include applying the 80/20 rule to focus on touchpoints that heavily influence the customer, separating frontstage from backstage touchpoints, and enriching the map with metrics and real customer quotes; it requires speaking to actual customers and is not suited to the ideation stage.
