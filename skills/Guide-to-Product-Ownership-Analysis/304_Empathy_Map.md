# Empathy Map

## Overview

A collaborative, human-centred visualization for getting "into the hearts and minds of customers," organized around six perspectives: Think & Feel (dominant emotions and unvoiced thoughts — was silence due to uncertainty, politeness, self-consciousness, or fear?), Hear (what friends, colleagues, and other channels tell them), See (what the customer sees during interaction — pleasing colour themes or confusing icons), Say & Do (verbatim quotes and observed actions, e.g., comparing prices on a comparison site), Pain (factors repelling purchase), and Gain (benefits and needs satisfied). The Guide distinguishes one-user maps, built from a single interview or diary study, from aggregated maps that represent a whole segment by combining maps of its customers; each persona or segment gets its own map.

## Context & Usage

Typically used during customer analysis and early in product delivery, driven by qualitative research; it shows practitioners which aspects of the customer they know and where they need more data. The Guide's session process: plan (choose participants — customers can join — tools, personas, and a shared goal), collect research (personas, journey maps, interviews, data reports), then complete the quadrants together, resolving disputed placements collectively and mining recurring themes, single-quadrant anomalies (a thought that was never said), and findings that fit no quadrant — usually a signal that further research is needed. Insight quality depends heavily on how much customers are willing to share, so validate with the customer present where possible.
