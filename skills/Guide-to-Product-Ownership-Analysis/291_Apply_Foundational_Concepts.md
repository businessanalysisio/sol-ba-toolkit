# Apply Foundational Concepts

## Overview

The first domain of the POA Framework, motivated by a stark statistic the Guide cites: industry research estimates more than 30,000 products are launched annually and close to 95% of them fail. The domain asks two diagnostic questions — "Is the organization set up to support product success?" and "Does the organization do a good job of setting up teams for success?" — and traces product failure to three organizational root causes: poorly defined interactions at the integration points between product management, Product Ownership, and product marketing; products misaligned with organizational strategy (products that "should never have been built"); and products poorly defined from the onset. Its remedy is a deliberate split of responsibilities across the three product disciplines — product management (lifecycle, vision, roadmap, release planning), Product Ownership (refine backlog items, ensure items are "ready", item acceptance, defect management), and product marketing (segmentation, positioning, business case development, pricing).

## Context & Usage

Applied before and during product build, at the strategy horizon rather than the iteration level. A POA Practitioner works in this domain when accountabilities blur between Product Owner and product manager, when leadership loses visibility of a product in development and misalignment creeps in, or when the defining artifacts — product vision, product roadmap, release plans — are stale or missing. The corrective moves are to define the key product disciplines, adopt an alignment strategy with regular touchpoints through to launch, and keep those artifacts current so teams build a well-defined product.
