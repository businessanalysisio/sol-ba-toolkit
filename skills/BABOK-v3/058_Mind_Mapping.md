# Mind Mapping

## Overview

Non-linear visual brainstorming technique that captures ideas radiating from a central topic. Mind maps organize complex information in a way that mirrors how the brain naturally associates concepts.

## Context & Usage

Applied during Elicitation (brainstorming requirements), RADD (organizing complex requirements), and anytime the BA needs to quickly capture and structure information. Start with a central topic; add major branches (main categories); then add sub-branches (details). Use colour and images to enhance recall. Excellent for workshop facilitation and personal note-taking.
