# Trustworthiness

## Overview

Inspiring confidence through consistent, honest, and reliable behaviour. Trustworthiness enables the BA to influence without authority and to gain the candid stakeholder input that quality requirements depend on.

## Context & Usage

Earned over time through repeated reliable behaviour. Critical during stakeholder engagement — stakeholders share their real needs only when they trust the BA. Also essential in negotiations, conflict resolution, and when managing sensitive organizational information.
