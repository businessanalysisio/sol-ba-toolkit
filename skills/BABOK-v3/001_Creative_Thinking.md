# Creative Thinking

## Overview

Generating novel approaches and solutions; not constrained by established patterns. The ability to think beyond 'how it has always been done' to discover new ways of addressing business needs.

## Context & Usage

Applied when existing approaches are insufficient, when innovation is needed, or when stakeholders are stuck in established thinking patterns. Use brainstorming and collaborative games to stimulate creative thinking in group settings. Particularly valuable in strategy analysis (defining future state) and solution option generation.
