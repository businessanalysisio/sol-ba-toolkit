# The Agile Perspective

## Overview

A just-in-time analysis approach — deliver the right detail at the right time, not everything upfront. The BA operates as a continuous team member, refining requirements iteratively through the product backlog.

## Context & Usage

Applied when the initiative uses an agile delivery methodology (Scrum, Kanban, SAFe). Key practices: user stories as requirements placeholder, continuous backlog refinement, acceptance criteria as definition of done, and close collaboration with the development team. The BA role shifts from phase-gate analyst to embedded team member focused on value delivery.
