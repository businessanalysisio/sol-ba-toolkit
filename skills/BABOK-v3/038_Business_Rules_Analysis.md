# Business Rules Analysis

## Overview

Extracting and defining constraints and directives that govern organizational behaviour. Business rules are atomic statements about how the business operates — they are not processes or requirements but constraints on both.

## Context & Usage

Applied during RADD when specifying requirements that must comply with policy, regulation, or business policy. Business rules should be stated declaratively ('A customer may have at most one active loan') independent of how they are implemented. Essential in regulated industries (banking, insurance, healthcare).
