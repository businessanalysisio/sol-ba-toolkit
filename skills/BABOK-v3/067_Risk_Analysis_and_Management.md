# Risk Analysis and Management

## Overview

Identifying, assessing, and planning responses to uncertainties that could affect the initiative. Risk analysis and management is proactive — it addresses what might go wrong before it does.

## Context & Usage

Applied in Strategy Analysis (assessing risks to future state), RADD (assessing solution option risks), and throughout the requirements life cycle (managing requirement volatility). Process: identify risks → assess probability × impact → classify (avoid/mitigate/transfer/accept) → plan response → monitor. Maintain a risk register throughout the initiative.
