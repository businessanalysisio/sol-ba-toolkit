# Verbal Communication

## Overview

Clear, concise, audience-adapted speaking. Verbal communication is the primary channel for elicitation, facilitation, presentation, and stakeholder management.

## Context & Usage

Used in every stakeholder interaction — interviews, workshops, presentations, informal conversations. Adapt vocabulary, pace, and level of detail to the audience (executives need summaries; SMEs need specifics). In workshops, verbal communication maintains focus and encourages participation.
