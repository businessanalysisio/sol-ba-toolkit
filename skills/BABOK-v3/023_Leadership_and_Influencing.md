# Leadership and Influencing

## Overview

Influencing without formal authority — creating alignment around goals and motivating stakeholders to act. Leadership in BA means driving the analysis process forward even when the BA doesn't manage the team.

## Context & Usage

Required when stakeholders are reluctant to participate, when decisions stall, when competing priorities need resolution, or when the BA must advocate for proper analysis practices. Builds on trustworthiness, business acumen, and communication skills. Senior BAs lead through influence, not title.
