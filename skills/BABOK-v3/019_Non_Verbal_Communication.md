# Non-Verbal Communication

## Overview

Reading body language, tone, and presence; adjusting one's own non-verbal signals. Non-verbal communication often conveys more than words, especially in emotionally charged situations.

## Context & Usage

Critical during facilitation and stakeholder engagement. Reading stakeholder posture, eye contact, and tone reveals resistance, confusion, or agreement that words may not express. The BA's own non-verbal signals (open posture, eye contact, calm tone) build trust and encourage candour.
