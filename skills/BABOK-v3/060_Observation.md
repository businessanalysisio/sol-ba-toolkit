# Observation

## Overview

Watching stakeholders perform their work (active or passive) to surface tacit knowledge — what people actually do versus what they say they do. Observation reveals workarounds, inefficiencies, and unstated needs.

## Context & Usage

Applied during Elicitation, especially for process-intensive domains or when stakeholders struggle to articulate their work. Types: passive (watch without interrupting), active (ask questions during observation), participant (perform the work yourself). Use when interviews have produced conflicting or incomplete information. Time-intensive but reveals insights no other technique can.
