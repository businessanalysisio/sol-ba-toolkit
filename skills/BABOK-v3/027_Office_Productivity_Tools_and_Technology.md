# Office Productivity Tools and Technology

## Overview

Proficient use of office productivity tools — word processors, spreadsheets, presentation software, diagramming tools. These are the foundational tools for documenting and communicating BA work.

## Context & Usage

Used daily for documenting requirements, creating traceability matrices, building presentations, and communicating analysis results. Spreadsheets are essential for managing large requirement sets, prioritization matrices, and RTMs. Diagramming tools produce process flows, data models, and wireframes.
