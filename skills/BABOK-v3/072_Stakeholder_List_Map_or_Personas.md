# Stakeholder List, Map, or Personas

## Overview

Identifying and characterizing stakeholders to plan engagement and understand their needs, influence, and attitudes. Deliverables include stakeholder lists, maps (Power/Interest Grid), and personas.

## Context & Usage

Applied during BA Planning and Monitoring (Plan Stakeholder Engagement) and throughout the initiative as new stakeholders emerge. The Power/Interest Grid classifies stakeholders into four quadrants: Manage Closely (high power, high interest), Keep Satisfied (high power, low interest), Keep Informed (low power, high interest), Monitor (low power, low interest). Personas add depth — describing goals, behaviours, and pain points of representative users.
