# Prioritization

## Overview

Ranking requirements by relative importance using defined criteria. Prioritization enables the team to focus on the most valuable work first and make defensible scope trade-offs.

## Context & Usage

Applied continuously in Requirements Life Cycle Management. Common techniques: MoSCoW (Must/Should/Could/Won't — simple and fast), Weighted Ranking (more rigorous, uses scored criteria), Timeboxing (fixed deadline, prioritise by value delivered), and Planning Poker (consensus-based estimation and prioritization). Prioritization criteria include: business value, risk, dependency, urgency, and cost.
