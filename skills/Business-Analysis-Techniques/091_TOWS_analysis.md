# TOWS analysis

## Overview

An extension of SWOT that cross-references the four quadrants to generate strategic options: SO ('maxi-maxi') strategies use strengths to exploit opportunities; WO ('mini-maxi') strategies overcome weaknesses to pursue opportunities; ST ('maxi-mini') strategies use strengths to counter threats; and WT ('mini-mini') strategies minimise weaknesses and avoid threats. Where SWOT describes position, TOWS drives action.

## Context & Usage

Applied after a SWOT has been completed, typically in a facilitated workshop during strategy analysis or options development, to convert analysis into candidate strategies. A BA uses TOWS to make sure a proposed initiative addresses real strategic pairings rather than a single quadrant in isolation. Tip: work systematically through each pairing of internal and external factors — the discipline of the matrix surfaces options that free-form discussion misses.
