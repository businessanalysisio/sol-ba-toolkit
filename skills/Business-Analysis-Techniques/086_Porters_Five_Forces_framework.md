# Porter's Five Forces framework

## Overview

Michael Porter's model for analysing the competitive intensity and attractiveness of an industry through five forces: the threat of new entrants, the bargaining power of suppliers, the bargaining power of buyers, the threat of substitute products or services, and the degree of rivalry among existing competitors. The stronger the forces, the harder it is to sustain profitability in that industry.

## Context & Usage

Used in strategy analysis to understand an organisation's industry-level environment, complementing the broader macro view given by PESTLE. A BA applies it when assessing strategic options, market entry decisions or the competitive rationale behind a change initiative. Tip: define the industry boundary carefully first — the analysis differs markedly depending on how narrowly the market is drawn — and use the findings to populate the opportunities and threats of a SWOT.
