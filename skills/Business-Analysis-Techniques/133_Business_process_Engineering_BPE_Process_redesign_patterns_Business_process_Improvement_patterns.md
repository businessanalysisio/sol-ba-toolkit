# Business process Engineering (BPE) / Process redesign patterns / Business process Improvement patterns

## Overview

A family of approaches for improving or radically re-engineering business processes by applying proven redesign patterns to an 'as-is' process model. Typical patterns include eliminating non-value-adding steps, simplifying or combining tasks, re-sequencing or parallelising activities, relocating work (e.g. to the customer or a shared service), redefining roles, and automating manual steps. BPE ranges from incremental improvement to fundamental 'clean sheet' redesign of end-to-end processes.

## Context & Usage

Applied after the current process has been modelled and analysed (usually with swimlane diagrams) and problems such as bottlenecks, hand-offs, delays and rework have been identified. The BA uses the patterns as a checklist to generate 'to-be' process options during the analyse-needs and solution-design stages, before feeding them into options evaluation and the business case. Practical tips: challenge every hand-off and approval step, measure cycle time versus work time to expose waiting, and validate redesigns against business events and customer outcomes rather than departmental convenience.
