# Options identification

## Overview

Generates the range of candidate ways to close an identified gap before any single solution is committed to. The mechanics run in two passes: first a deliberately long list produced by brainstorming without evaluation, then a filtering pass against knock-out criteria (budget ceilings, policy constraints, mandatory deadlines) to reach a shortlist — typically around three options — for detailed evaluation. Options usually vary along two dimensions: scope (which processes, functions or business areas are included) and solution approach (change the process only, adopt a packaged product, build bespoke, outsource). The "do nothing" or minimum-change option is always retained as the baseline every other option is compared against.

## Context & Usage

Sits between gap analysis and the business case: without a genuine spread of options, the business case degenerates into justifying a decision already made. Facilitated workshops with a mixed business and technical audience produce the strongest long lists; the BA's job is to keep premature evaluation out of the generation session and to make incremental and radical options coexist on the list. Each shortlisted option is then carried forward into feasibility screening and cost-benefit work.
