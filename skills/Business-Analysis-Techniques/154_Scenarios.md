# Scenarios

## Overview

Tells the story of one actor pursuing one goal through the proposed solution, step by step from a defined starting condition to a defined outcome — "Mrs Patel phones to change her delivery address after the order has shipped" rather than "the system shall support address changes". A worked scenario identifies the actor, the trigger, any preconditions, the sequence of steps in the normal course, and the end state; the analysis then deliberately probes the alternatives at every step ("what if the payment is declined? what if the order is already out for delivery?"), producing alternate and exception paths that abstract requirements statements reliably miss.

## Context & Usage

Deployed during elicitation and validation because concrete narratives unlock stakeholder knowledge that generalisations cannot — people who struggle to enumerate rules will instantly correct a story that gets their work wrong. Scenarios flesh out use case descriptions (main and alternate flows), give user stories their acceptance dimension, script prototypes and walkthroughs, and convert directly into test cases. Ground them in specifics: named personas, realistic data, actual documents. The vaguer the scenario, the less it flushes out.
