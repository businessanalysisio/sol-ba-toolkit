# Hothousing

## Overview

An intensive, competitive workshop format for defining and proving a solution in days rather than months. Mixed teams of business users and developers work in parallel on the same problem within a compressed event — typically two to five consecutive days — building prototype solutions in rapid iterations and pitching each iteration to a judging panel of senior stakeholders who score it against agreed criteria and feed back immediately. The competitive pressure and the presence of empowered decision-makers in the room collapse the usual elicit-document-review-revise cycle: requirements are clarified, options tested and design decisions taken inside the event itself.

## Context & Usage

Suited to situations where speed matters more than polish — proving whether a concept is worth funding, unblocking a stalled requirements effort, or kick-starting an agile delivery with a validated backlog. Success depends on preparation: a crisply framed problem statement, the right mix of participants released full-time for the duration, technical environments ready on day one, and judges with genuine authority to decide. The follow-through is the common failure point, so winning ideas need named owners and funded next steps agreed before the event closes.
