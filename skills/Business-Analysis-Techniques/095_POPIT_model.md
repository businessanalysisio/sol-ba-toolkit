# POPIT™ model

## Overview

A holistic view of a business system associated with UK BCS business analysis practice (a trademark of Assist Knowledge Development), whose letters stand for People, Organisation, Processes, Information and Technology. Rather than analysing a change through an IT lens alone, POPIT insists every dimension be considered: do people have the right skills and motivation, does the organisation provide fitting structure and culture, are processes well designed, does information flow to where decisions are made, and does technology support all of the above. The model underpins holistic gap analysis — comparing current and target states element by element.

## Context & Usage

Applied when scoping business change options and when converting an agreed target state into a complete set of change activities. A common pattern: after building a Business Activity Model and running gap analysis, the BA organises the identified gaps under the POPIT headings so that people, process and organisational actions are planned alongside the IT build. It also serves as a review checklist — a business case or solution design that addresses only one or two POPIT elements is a warning sign.
