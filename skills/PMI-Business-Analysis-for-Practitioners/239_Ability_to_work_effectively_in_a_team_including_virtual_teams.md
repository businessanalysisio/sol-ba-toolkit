# Ability to work effectively in a team (including virtual teams)

## Overview

Operating as a reliable node in a delivery team — sharing information before being asked, honoring commitments to teammates as firmly as commitments to sponsors, and flexing across role boundaries when the work demands it — with an added layer of craft for distributed teams. Virtual collaboration changes the BA's mechanics specifically: elicitation moves from whiteboards to shared living documents and collaborative modeling tools; ambiguity that a co-located developer would resolve by leaning over a desk must instead be caught by writing requirements to a higher standard of self-sufficiency; and time-zone spreads mean the BA deliberately schedules requirement clarifications into the narrow overlap hours while pushing everything survivable into asynchronous channels. Trust builds differently at distance — through consistent written responsiveness rather than hallway presence — and silence from a remote stakeholder is a warning sign, not consent.

## Context & Usage

Business Analysis Planning should state explicitly how a distributed team will elicit, review, and approve requirements — tooling, response-time norms, and which decisions require synchronous sessions. Requirements Elicitation & Analysis feels the virtual tax hardest: remote workshops need shorter agendas, more structure, and deliberate turn-taking to keep quiet participants contributing. In Traceability & Monitoring, a shared requirements repository becomes the team's single source of truth precisely because there is no physical wall of sticky notes to walk past.
