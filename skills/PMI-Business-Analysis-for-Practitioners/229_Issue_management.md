# Issue management

## Overview

The discipline of capturing, tracking, and driving to closure the problems that have already materialized — distinct from risks, which might. For the BA this means a live issue log where every entry carries an owner, a severity, a needed-by date, and a description precise enough that resolution is verifiable ("SME for tax rules unavailable until Q3, blocking requirements R-041 to R-058" — not "resource problems"). The hard part is not logging but escalation timing: an unresolved requirements conflict left to age quietly becomes a build blocker discovered in testing, while over-escalating trivia teaches management to ignore your log. Weekly review of aging open items is the minimum heartbeat.

## Context & Usage

Issues cluster in Requirements Elicitation & Analysis (unavailable stakeholders, contradictory answers, missing source documents) and in Traceability & Monitoring, where PMI treats unresolved requirement conflicts and change disputes as items requiring managed disposition, not hope. Needs Assessment produces its own issues — disputed baseline data, disagreement over problem framing — that must be closed before the situation statement is credible. Every open issue that touches a requirement should be linked to it, so the RTM shows which requirements are built on unstable ground.
