# Business subject matter expert

## Overview

The domain-truth holder — a senior claims adjuster, a customs compliance officer, a warehouse shift lead — seconded to change work part-time or full-time because they carry knowledge that exists nowhere on paper. BA work manifests through the SME rather than by them: they supply the edge cases ("that rule flips for policies written before 2019"), walk requirement scenarios to check they survive contact with reality, validate models, and act as the arbiter when two elicitation sources conflict. Working with SMEs has known hazards the BA must manage: experts describe exceptions as if they were the rule and the current system's behavior as if it were the requirement; a single SME's view gets mistaken for the whole department's; and the best SMEs are the people operations can least spare, so their scarce hours must be spent on judgment calls, not on information a document already holds.

## Context & Usage

PMI counts SMEs among those performing business analysis activities, chiefly inside Requirements Elicitation & Analysis as the primary elicitation source and the reviewer whose sign-off makes validation meaningful. In Needs Assessment, SMEs sanity-check root cause hypotheses against how work actually happens. During Solution Evaluation they are often the earliest sensor that a delivered solution fails in practice — the workaround an SME invents in week two is evaluation data no survey will capture.
