# Product manager / product owner

## Overview

The role that owns the value proposition and the backlog priority — deciding not just what gets built but what deliberately does not, and answering for the commercial result. BA work shows up in this role as acceptance criteria (the moment a product owner writes "done means the refund posts within one business day," they are specifying a requirement) and as roadmap trade-offs, where every quarter's plan is a prioritization exercise weighing customer evidence, revenue, technical debt, and strategic bets against a fixed team capacity. The vantage differs from a project BA's: needs come from the market — churn data, support tickets, win/loss interviews, competitor moves — rather than from a sponsor's mandate, and being wrong shows up in revenue, not in a variance report. Where the team also has a dedicated BA, the PO owns the "what and why" ordering while the BA deepens the "what exactly"; the pairing fails when both assume the other is writing the acceptance criteria.

## Context & Usage

PMI recognizes product managers and product owners among the roles performing business analysis, and the mapping to its domains is continuous rather than phased: Needs Assessment is ongoing market and user discovery; BA Planning becomes release and refinement cadence; Requirements Elicitation & Analysis happens through backlog refinement and customer conversations; Traceability & Monitoring links roadmap themes to epics to stories; Solution Evaluation is the product metrics review — adoption, retention, revenue per feature — that feeds directly back into next quarter's priorities.
