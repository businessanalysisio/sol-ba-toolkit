# Enterprise business analyst

## Overview

The BA who works before and above projects: engaged when an executive says "something is wrong with retention" and finished (in one sense) when a funded, scoped initiative exists. BA work in this role manifests upstream of delivery — quantifying the problem into a situation statement, running the root cause and opportunity analysis, developing options with rough-order costs and benefits, and assembling the business case a portfolio board can fund or kill. The role demands comfort with ambiguity and thin data: analysis at this altitude runs on interviews with executives, financial extracts, and market context rather than detailed process evidence, and its recommendations shape where millions go. A distinctive obligation is intellectual honesty about the "do nothing" option, which enterprise politics always pressures toward omission.

## Context & Usage

Of PMI's roles performing business analysis, this one owns Needs Assessment nearly end-to-end — the domain's activities read as this role's job description. It also seeds Business Analysis Planning by handing the project BA a business case, scope framing, and stakeholder landscape to build on; a clean handoff here prevents the classic failure where delivery teams never learn why the project exists. The role returns for Solution Evaluation, checking realized benefits against the case it wrote — accountability that keeps future business cases honest.
