# Familiarity with multiple project and development methodologies

## Overview

Fluency across predictive (waterfall), iterative, and adaptive (agile) delivery approaches, plus the judgment to match BA practice to whichever one the project uses. A fixed-price, regulated integration with an external vendor genuinely needs a baselined requirements specification signed before build; a product team exploring an unproven market genuinely needs a story backlog refined just-in-time — and applying either regime to the other's context is a classic BA failure. The competency covers the vocabulary and ceremonies of each world (BRDs, stage gates, change boards; epics, sprints, definition of ready) and the ability to scale documentation up or down without losing rigor: an agile team still needs acceptance criteria, just not a 200-page document.

## Context & Usage

This knowledge is exercised primarily in Business Analysis Planning, where PMI has the BA select deliverable types, elicitation cadence, and traceability formality based on the project life cycle chosen. It reshapes Requirements Elicitation & Analysis (big-batch specification versus rolling backlog refinement), Traceability & Monitoring (formal RTM and change board versus backlog links and reprioritization), and Solution Evaluation (end-of-project acceptance versus per-increment review). BAs who know only one methodology tend to fight the project instead of serving it.
