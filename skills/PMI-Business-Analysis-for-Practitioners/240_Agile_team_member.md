# Agile team member

## Overview

A role, not a title: the person doing business analysis from inside a Scrum or Kanban team, frequently without "BA" on their badge. Here BA work manifests as backlog refinement — slicing epics into stories thin enough to finish in days, writing Given/When/Then acceptance criteria just-in-time rather than months ahead, and being the teammate who answers a developer's "what should happen if the coupon expired mid-checkout?" the same afternoon so the sprint does not stall. The role trades the standalone requirements document for a continuously groomed backlog plus conversation, and it demands comfort with deliberate incompleteness: detail is added at the last responsible moment, and analysis running more than a sprint or two ahead of build is treated as inventory, not progress.

## Context & Usage

PMI names the agile team member among the roles that perform business analysis, and all five domains persist in compressed form: Needs Assessment lives in the product vision and story "so that" clauses; BA Planning becomes definition-of-ready and refinement cadence; Requirements Elicitation & Analysis happens per-story in refinement sessions and three-amigos conversations; Traceability & Monitoring is carried by backlog linkage from epic to story to test; Solution Evaluation runs every sprint review, where working software is checked against real user need rather than a signed document.
