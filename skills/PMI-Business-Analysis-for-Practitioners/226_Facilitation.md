# Facilitation

## Overview

Designing and running group sessions that produce a defined output — not just "a good discussion." A facilitating BA writes the agenda backwards from the artifact the session must yield (a prioritized list, a signed-off process model, a resolved conflict), opens with ground rules, uses a parking lot to capture off-topic issues without losing them, and actively manages airtime so the senior manager in the room does not become the only voice heard. Techniques like round-robin input, silent brainstorming, and dot voting exist precisely to counteract group dynamics. Neutrality is the defining discipline: the moment participants sense the facilitator is steering toward a preferred answer, they stop contributing honestly.

## Context & Usage

Requirements Elicitation & Analysis is facilitation's home domain — PMI treats facilitated workshops as one of the highest-bandwidth elicitation techniques, and prioritization sessions, model walkthroughs, and requirement reviews all live or die on facilitation quality. In Needs Assessment, facilitated sessions surface competing views of the problem before analysis begins. In Solution Evaluation, structured retrospectives and evaluation workshops need the same discipline. BA Planning should explicitly budget preparation time: an unprepared workshop wastes the most expensive hour on the calendar — everyone's at once.
