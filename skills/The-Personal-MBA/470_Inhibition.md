# Inhibition

## Overview

Inhibition is the mind's ability to temporarily override a default response — to not eat the cookie, not fire off the angry reply, not blurt the first offer in a negotiation. This is what willpower actually is mechanically: a veto power over impulses, exercised by the newest and most energy-hungry part of the brain. Crucially, it is a temporary override, not a permanent rewrite; the underlying impulse remains and reasserts itself the moment vigilance lapses.

## Context & Usage

Budget inhibition for short, high-stakes bursts and never build a system that depends on it continuously. It is the right tool for pausing before responding to a hostile client email or holding silence after naming your price; it is the wrong tool for month-after-month resistance of a badly designed environment. When you find yourself inhibiting the same impulse daily — checking metrics obsessively, micro-managing a hire — that is a signal to change the guiding structure rather than keep paying the override cost.
