# Point of Market Entry

## Overview

For most offers there are predictable moments in people's lives when they suddenly become interested in a category — Kaufman calls these Points of Market Entry. Expecting parents enter the market for cribs, strollers, and baby gear; newlyweds enter the market for homes and insurance; new graduates enter the market for professional wardrobes and first apartments. Before that triggering life event, these people ignore your marketing entirely; at the moment of entry, they become intensely receptive and actively searching. The strategic insight is that being present and visible precisely when a customer crosses this threshold is worth far more than generic year-round advertising, because you capture them at the peak of their receptivity — and often win their loyalty before competitors even appear.

## Context & Usage

Identify your customers' points of market entry when planning where and when to invest marketing effort. Rather than advertising constantly to a broad audience, position yourself to be found at the trigger moment — a baby-products brand that partners with prenatal classes and obstetricians reaches parents exactly as they enter the market. Search advertising is powerful here because it catches people in the act of entering (searching "best stroller" the week a baby is due). Map the life events or business milestones that switch on demand for your category, then arrange to be the first, most helpful presence at that entry point.
