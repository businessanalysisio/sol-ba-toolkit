# Willpower Depletion

## Overview

Willpower behaves like a muscle: it fatigues with use over the course of a day and recovers with rest. Kaufman cites Roy Baumeister's classic experiment — subjects who had to resist fresh cookies and eat radishes instead gave up on a subsequent puzzle in half the time of those who ate the cookies. Every act of inhibition draws down the same shared reserve, which is why evenings are when diets break, budgets get blown, and ill-advised emails get sent.

## Context & Usage

Sequence your day and your systems around a finite reserve. Do the work requiring the most discipline — prospecting calls, hard writing, pricing decisions — early, before depletion sets in; schedule contract reviews and negotiations when you're fresh, never at the end of a draining day. Better still, spend willpower once to change the structure (unsubscribe, automate, remove the option) instead of spending it daily on resistance. For teams: a policy that requires constant self-restraint from staff is a policy that will be violated.
