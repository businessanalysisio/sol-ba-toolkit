# Mental Simulation

## Overview

The mind's built-in flight simulator: the ability to imagine a future course of action and pre-experience its consequences without paying the real-world cost. Daydreaming about "what would happen if..." is not idle — it is the brain running predictive scenarios drawn from stored patterns, which is why you can rehearse a difficult conversation, feel the objections coming, and adjust your approach before ever entering the room.

## Context & Usage

Run simulations before expensive commitments. Walk mentally through a customer's first ten minutes with your product and notice where they get stuck; rehearse the investor pitch including the three hardest questions; imagine month six of the partnership you're about to sign, including the failure mode. Simulation is cheapest early — an hour imagining the warehouse workflow catches layout problems that would cost weeks to fix after the racking is bolted down. Its accuracy depends entirely on the pattern library feeding it, so simulate in domains where you have real exposure.
