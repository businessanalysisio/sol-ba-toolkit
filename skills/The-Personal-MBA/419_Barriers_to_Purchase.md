# Barriers to Purchase

## Overview

Barriers to Purchase are the small set of standard objections that stop an interested prospect from actually buying. Kaufman enumerates the common ones: (1) "It costs too much," (2) "It won't work," (3) "It won't work for me specifically," (4) "I can wait — I don't need it now," and (5) "It's too difficult to set up or use." Nearly every stalled sale traces to one of these, and the value of the list is that it turns a vague "they didn't buy" into a diagnosable problem. Each barrier has known antidotes — price framing and guarantees for cost, social proof and demos for "it won't work," case studies from similar customers for "not for me," a compelling now-reason for "I can wait," and onboarding or done-for-you setup for "too difficult." The point is that objections are predictable, so you can pre-empt them inside the offer rather than scramble in the moment.

## Context & Usage

Use the list as a checklist when designing a sales page, pitch, or offer: walk through all five barriers and make sure each is answered before the prospect raises it. Example: a SaaS company seeing high trial signups but low conversion maps the drop-off to barrier five ("too difficult") and adds guided onboarding and migration help, lifting conversions without touching price. When a specific deal stalls, identify which barrier is actually blocking it — treating a "won't work for me" objection with a discount (a "costs too much" fix) burns margin and misses the real concern.
