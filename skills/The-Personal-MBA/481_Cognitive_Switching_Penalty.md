# Cognitive Switching Penalty

## Overview

Every time attention moves between contexts, the brain pays a toll: the old context must be swapped out and the new one loaded — relevant facts, goals, and mental state reassembled from scratch. The penalty is paid on every switch, so a day of constant task-hopping can consume most of its hours in reload overhead while feeling intensely busy. Multitasking is not parallel processing; it is rapid serial switching with the toll charged at every turn.

## Context & Usage

Batch and serialize. Group similar small tasks — email, invoicing, approvals — into dedicated blocks so the context loads once and amortizes across many items; handle projects in longer exclusive sessions rather than round-robin slices. Diagnostically, when a capable person or team is working hard but shipping little, count their daily context switches before questioning their competence: five concurrent projects at 20% each yields far less than five projects done sequentially, because the fractions exclude the switching tax. Limit work-in-progress accordingly.
