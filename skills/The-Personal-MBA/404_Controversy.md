# Controversy

## Overview

Controversy is deliberately taking a provocative or polarizing stance to attract attention and galvanize an audience. Kaufman presents it as a high-risk, high-reward marketing tactic: taking a clear position that some people strongly support and others strongly oppose generates conversation, coverage, and passionate loyalty from those who agree. The mechanism is that controversy draws attention precisely because it creates opposing camps — people argue, share, and take sides, spreading your message far beyond what neutral content would reach. The trade-off is real: controversy repels part of the audience and can damage reputation if mishandled or if the stance is inauthentic. Used honestly and in service of genuine beliefs, though, it strengthens the bond with your core supporters even as it alienates people who were never going to buy anyway.

## Context & Usage

Consider controversy when you want to stand out sharply and are willing to accept that not everyone will like you. A brand that takes a firm, values-driven public stand energizes customers who share those values into vocal advocates, while those who disagree simply weren't its market. The discipline is authenticity and proportion — manufactured or reckless controversy tends to backfire and erode trust, whereas a sincere position aligned with your narrative deepens it. Weigh the audience you'll energize against the audience you'll lose, and only court controversy when the trade is favorable and the stance is one you genuinely hold.
