# Pattern Matching

## Overview

The brain is fundamentally a pattern-recognition machine: it stores patterns from every experience and matches new situations against that library to predict what will happen next. Expertise, in Kaufman's framing, is nothing mystical — it is simply a very large, well-organized collection of stored patterns that lets a veteran "see" in seconds what a novice cannot see at all. The quality of your automatic judgment is capped by the quantity and variety of patterns you have fed your brain.

## Context & Usage

Treat pattern acquisition as a deliberate business investment. Before pricing your first consulting engagement, study fifty real proposals; before acquiring a company, read post-mortems of failed acquisitions; before designing an onboarding flow, walk through twenty competitors'. An experienced retail buyer who "just knows" a product will flop is running pattern matching on years of SKU-level exposure — a founder can compress that timeline by studying cases intensively rather than waiting for slow personal experience.
