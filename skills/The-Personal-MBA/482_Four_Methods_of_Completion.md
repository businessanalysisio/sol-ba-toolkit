# Four Methods of Completion

## Overview

There are exactly four ways to get anything off your plate: completion (do it yourself), deletion (decide it doesn't need doing and drop it), delegation (have someone else do it), and deferment (consciously postpone it until later). Kaufman's insight is that only tasks genuinely requiring your unique skills deserve the first method — most items on a real to-do list are better served by the other three, yet completion is the default people reach for on everything.

## Context & Usage

Run every backlog through the four gates. Triaging an inbox of forty requests: perhaps eight need your hands, ten can be declined outright, twelve belong with teammates or contractors, and ten can wait for a scheduled review. Founders drowning in operational work are usually mis-sorting — completing tasks that should be deleted ("do we even need this report?") or delegated (bookkeeping, scheduling). Deletion is the most underused gate and the highest-leverage one: the fastest task is the one you stop pretending to owe.
