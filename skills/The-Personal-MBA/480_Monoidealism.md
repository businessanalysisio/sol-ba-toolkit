# Monoidealism

## Overview

The state of holding a single idea in mind without internal conflict or distraction — Kaufman's term for what athletes and creators call flow. When attention is undivided and no competing desire is tugging at the controls, work feels effortless and output quality jumps. Monoidealism is fragile by nature: one notification, one unresolved worry, one "quick question" collapses it, and rebuilding the state takes far longer than breaking it did.

## Context & Usage

Engineer the conditions rather than hoping for the mood. Close the loops that would otherwise intrude (capture open tasks in a trusted list), remove the interruption vectors (phone in another room, chat status off), and give the target task a clear, singular definition before starting — vague goals invite the mind to wander among alternatives. For knowledge-work teams, protect contiguous blocks: a maker's four uninterrupted hours produce more than eight hours diced by meetings, because only the former permits monoideal focus.
