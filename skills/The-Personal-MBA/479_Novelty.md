# Novelty

## Overview

Attention is captured by what is new and fades from what is constant — the brain habituates to any unchanging stimulus and stops reporting it. The billboard you passed every day for a month is invisible by week two; the ad creative that converted brilliantly in March is wallpaper by June. Novelty is the reason attention must be re-earned continuously: no message, however good, holds an audience without variation.

## Context & Usage

Plan for habituation instead of being surprised by it. Rotate ad creative before performance decays, refresh storefronts and email formats on a schedule, and give existing customers periodic news — new features, new packaging, new stories — because a static offer slowly exits their awareness even if quality never drops. Balance against its cost: novelty-chasing that changes the core product confuses customers, so keep the promise stable and refresh the presentation. Habituation also works for you — an initially uncomfortable price increase becomes the unremarkable norm within months.
