# Five-Fold How

## Overview

The descending counterpart of Five-Fold Why: take a large, abstract intention and repeatedly ask "how, concretely?" until you arrive at a physical action you could take today. "Achieve financial independence" → how? "Build income beyond my salary" → how? "Launch a productized service" → how? "Validate demand with ten prospect conversations" → how? "Email three former colleagues this afternoon." Where the why-chain ascends to meaning, the how-chain descends to motion — a goal that cannot survive five hows is a wish, not a plan.

## Context & Usage

Deploy it whenever a goal has been sitting inert. Strategy offsites routinely end with intentions like "become the category leader in mid-market"; run the how-chain in the room until every priority terminates in a named person doing a specific thing by a specific date, or admit the priority is decorative. Individually, the chain is the antidote to overwhelm: a project that feels too big to start feels that way because it's being held at the wrong altitude — five hows down, there is always a next step small enough to be unintimidating.
