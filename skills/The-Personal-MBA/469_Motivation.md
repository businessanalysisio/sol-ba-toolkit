# Motivation

## Overview

Motivation is the emotional energy that links desire to action, and it runs in two directions: moving toward something desirable or moving away from something threatening. Away-from motivation is more urgent but burns out once the threat recedes; toward motivation sustains longer effort. Kaufman's practical twist: motivation is more often a result of action than a prerequisite for it — start moving, however small the step, and the feeling tends to follow, not the other way around.

## Context & Usage

Two applications. Personally: when you're stalled, stop waiting to "feel like it" — take a five-minute starter action and let momentum generate the motivation. In marketing and management: identify which direction drives your audience. Insurance, security software, and compliance services sell on away-from energy (avoid the disaster); travel, education, and fitness sell on toward energy (become the future self). Pitching a fear-driven buyer with aspiration copy, or vice versa, wastes the message.
