# Guiding Structure

## Overview

The structure of your environment shapes your behavior far more reliably than willpower does. Kaufman's rule: if you want to change a behavior, don't fight it head-on — change the structure that produces it. The canonical example is dieting: keeping junk food out of the house beats resisting the junk food sitting on the counter, because the environment no longer presents the choice at all. Structure wins every contest it has with intention.

## Context & Usage

Reach for this model when a person or team keeps "failing" at a behavior despite genuine commitment — the fix is almost always structural, not motivational. A founder who checks email compulsively should remove the mail app from the phone; a team that skips code review should make merging impossible without one; a store that wants larger baskets should place staples at the back. Before blaming discipline, ask: what does the current structure make easy, and what does it make hard?
