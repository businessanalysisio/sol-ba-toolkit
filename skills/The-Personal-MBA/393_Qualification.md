# Qualification

## Overview

Qualification is the act of screening prospects to determine which ones are worth pursuing before you invest significant time and effort in them. Not every interested party is a good customer — some can't afford the offer, some don't have real authority to buy, some aren't a genuine fit and will only drain resources or become unhappy. Kaufman frames qualification as a filter that concentrates your limited selling energy on the prospects most likely to convert into satisfied, paying customers. Good qualification questions surface budget, need, authority, and fit early, so you can gracefully decline poor matches and focus on strong ones. Counterintuitively, turning away the wrong prospects makes the business stronger, because mismatched customers cost more than they're worth.

## Context & Usage

Build qualification into the top of your sales process whenever demand exceeds the time you can spend on each lead, or when bad-fit customers are causing trouble. A consultant who asks about budget and decision timeline in the first call avoids weeks spent courting someone who was never going to buy. Qualification also improves the customer's experience — steering someone toward a better-fitting solution (even a competitor's) builds trust and reputation. Use it to protect your capacity for the probable purchasers who will actually benefit and pay.
