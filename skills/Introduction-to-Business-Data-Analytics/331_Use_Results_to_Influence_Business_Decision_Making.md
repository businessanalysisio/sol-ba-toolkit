# Use Results to Influence Business Decision-Making

## Overview

The fifth and final domain of the lifecycle: operationalizing findings so they actually change what the business does. It covers recommending solution options weighed against feasibility and value, developing an implementation plan for the analytics-driven change, managing the people-side transition so decisions stick, and closing the loop by monitoring whether the acted-on insight moved the intended metric. This domain is what distinguishes analytics that informs a decision from analytics that produces a report nobody uses — the deliverable is a business outcome, not a chart.

## Context & Usage

Sits last in the cycle (identify → source → analyze → interpret & report → use), and often re-triggers the cycle as new questions surface. Concrete example: given the delivery-delay churn finding, this domain means recommending options (expedite the premium segment's fulfillment vs. proactively credit delayed orders), building the rollout and ownership plan, managing operations-team adoption of the new shipping SLA, and then tracking premium-segment retention over the next two quarters to confirm the change worked.
