# Analytical storytelling / data storytelling

## Overview

Weaving data, visuals, and narrative into a structured account that moves an audience from situation to insight to recommended action. Where a chart shows a fact, a data story gives it meaning and momentum: it sets context (what was the question and why it matters), presents the evidence in a deliberate sequence, names the insight plainly, addresses the "so what," and lands on what should happen next. Effective storytelling foregrounds the one message that drives the decision, keeps supporting detail in reserve, and is honest about uncertainty — persuading through clarity rather than by hiding caveats.

## Context & Usage

The culminating interpret-and-report activity, drawing on visualization and communication-needs analysis and handing directly to the Use domain (identify → source → analyze → interpret & report → use). Concrete example: the churn story opens with the $2M premium-retention stakes, walks through the delay-versus-churn evidence, states the insight ("our most valuable customers are also our slowest-served and most at-risk"), and closes on the recommended action — expedite premium fulfillment — so the sponsor leaves not with a chart but with a decision to make.
