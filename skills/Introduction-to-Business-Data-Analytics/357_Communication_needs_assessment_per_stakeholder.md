# Communication-needs assessment per stakeholder

## Overview

Determining, for each stakeholder, what they need to hear about the analysis, in what form, at what depth, and how often — so findings land rather than get lost. It profiles each audience on their decision role, data literacy, preferred format (dashboard, one-page brief, detailed appendix, live walkthrough), tolerance for technical detail, and the specific decision they must make. The output is a communication plan that pairs message, medium, and cadence to audience, ensuring an executive gets the "so what" while a technical peer gets the methodology and caveats.

## Context & Usage

An interpret-and-report activity, following stakeholder identification and shaping how visualizations and the narrative are built (identify → source → analyze → interpret & report → use). Concrete example: for the churn findings, the assessment specifies that the VP sponsor gets a single slide with the retained-revenue opportunity and one recommended action, the operations lead gets the segment-level delay breakdown they can act on, and the data team gets the model spec and validation results — one analysis, three deliberately different renderings.
