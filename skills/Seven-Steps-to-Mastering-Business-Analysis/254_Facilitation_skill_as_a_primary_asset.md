# Facilitation skill as a primary asset

## Overview

Carkenord ranks facilitation among the BA's most valuable assets because so much of the work happens in groups: eliciting requirements, resolving conflicting needs, reviewing documents, reaching sign-off. The core stance is neutrality — the facilitator guides the process (agenda, participation, decisions captured) while the group owns the content, which means resisting the temptation to supply answers yourself. Concrete moves include drawing out quiet participants by name, containing dominators without embarrassing them, parking off-topic issues visibly, and closing every session by restating what was decided and what happens next.

## Context & Usage

Reach for it in requirements workshops, prioritization sessions, and any meeting where consensus is the deliverable rather than information transfer. It matters most when stakeholders disagree: a facilitated structure (agree on criteria first, debate positions second) keeps conflict productive. New BAs should build the muscle in low-stakes meetings before facilitating a room with executives in it.
