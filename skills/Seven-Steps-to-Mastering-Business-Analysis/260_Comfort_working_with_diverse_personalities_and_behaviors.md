# Comfort working with diverse personalities and behaviors

## Overview

A BA cannot choose their stakeholders: the same week may include a data-hungry analyst who wants every figure sourced, an expressive marketer who thinks aloud in tangents, a driver executive who wants the answer in one sentence, and a skeptic who was burned by the last project. Carkenord's point is that effectiveness comes from adjusting your own style to each of them — pace, level of detail, small talk or none — rather than expecting them to adapt to you, and from not taking difficult behavior personally: resistance is usually about the change, the history, or the workload, not about the analyst.

## Context & Usage

Draw on this whenever a stakeholder relationship feels stuck: diagnose the style mismatch before assuming bad faith. Practical reads include how someone communicates (email essays vs. one-liners), decides (data vs. gut), and handles conflict (direct vs. avoidant), then meet them there. It underpins elicitation especially — a technique that works on a talkative SME (open interview) fails on a reticent one, who may respond better to reviewing a draft model and correcting it.
