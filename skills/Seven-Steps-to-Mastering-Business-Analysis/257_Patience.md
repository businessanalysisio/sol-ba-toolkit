# Patience

## Overview

Patience for a BA is a working method, not a temperament: resisting the urge to jump to solutions, allowing stakeholders the time they need to articulate needs they may never have put into words before, and tolerating the fact that requirements mature through iterative refinement rather than emerging complete on the first pass. Carkenord calls it out because analysis pressure runs the other way — sponsors want dates, developers want specs, and the fastest route to a wrong system is agreeing to the first plausible answer. Patience also shows up as comfort with silence in interviews; the pause after a question is often where the real answer forms.

## Context & Usage

Exercise it when a stakeholder rambles toward their point, when the third revision of a requirement still isn't right, and when a solution idea arrives before the problem is understood — acknowledge the idea, park it, and return to the need behind it. Patience is not passivity: pair it with tenacity, so that giving people time never turns into letting open questions drift unanswered.
