# Planning, Designing, and Building a Website

## Overview

A business website comes together in a fixed order: define its job (brochure, lead capture, or full e-commerce), register a domain that matches the business name, choose a build route — website builder (Wix/Squarespace, hours to launch), CMS (WordPress, more control), or custom development (highest cost and flexibility) — then structure pages around what a visitor needs in the first few seconds: what you sell, why you, and a clear call to action. Non-negotiables regardless of route: mobile-responsive layout (most retail traffic now arrives on phones), page load under roughly three seconds before abandonment climbs, an SSL certificate, a legally required privacy/cookie notice, and visible contact details. Treat the site as an ongoing cost line — hosting, domain renewal, content updates, security patches — not a one-off build.

## Context & Usage

Plan the website alongside brand development and before launch marketing, because every campaign will point traffic at it. Whether building yourself or hiring a developer, start from a sitemap and a written brief (audience, goal per page, content owner); the brief is what keeps a freelance build on budget. After launch, review analytics monthly to find where visitors drop off and iterate page by page.
