# Attracting Website Traffic

## Overview

Traffic arrives through four channels with very different economics: organic search (SEO — keyword-targeted pages, backlinks, and technical health; slow to build, cheap to sustain), paid search and display (instant volume, cost-per-click that stops the moment spend stops), social and content (blogs, video, email newsletters that compound as an owned audience), and referral (links from partners, directories, and press). SEO mechanics matter concretely: title tags and page copy matched to the phrases customers actually type, fast load times, and inbound links from reputable sites are the main ranking levers, and the first page of results captures the overwhelming majority of clicks. The discipline is to measure each channel by cost per visitor and conversion rate rather than raw volume, since a thousand mistargeted visitors are worth less than fifty qualified ones.

## Context & Usage

Turn to this once the website exists and launch approaches — typically pairing a paid burst for immediate visibility with SEO and content groundwork that pays off over 6–12 months. Review the channel mix monthly against analytics: which sources bring visitors who buy, subscribe, or enquire, and shift budget toward those.
