# Agentra Product Map

Agentra Product Map là bản đồ liên kết các sản phẩm, workflow, content system và BA methodology trong hệ sinh thái [[agentra]].

## Hệ sinh thái

| Layer | Thành phần | Vai trò | Wiki link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform | Agentra | Workflow AI cho giáo dục | [[agentra]] |
| Teacher product | EduFlow | Soạn bài STEM từ ma trận | [[eduflow]] |
| Student/community product | OpenEdu | Luyện tập mở, riêng tư | [[openedu]] |
| Methodology | BA Toolkit | Phân tích yêu cầu, workflow, traceability, risk | [[business-analysis-toolkit]] |
| Content engine | Agentic content ops | Research -> content -> analytics loop | [[agentic-content-operations]] |

## Product Relationships

```text
Agentra
├── EduFlow: teacher workflow and academic document generation
├── OpenEdu: student practice and community question bank
├── BA Toolkit: requirements and workflow intelligence
└── Content System: market education and go-to-market learning
```

## Product Boundaries

| Product | Does | Does not |
|---|---|---|
| [[agentra]] | Orchestrates human-approved education AI workflows | Act like a generic chatbot |
| [[eduflow]] | Generates synchronized STEM lesson materials | Replace teacher judgment |
| [[openedu]] | Provides free no-login practice | Track students through central profiles |
| [[business-analysis-toolkit]] | Structures requirements, risks and processes | Serve as end-user learning app |
