# Agentra Content Pillars From Business Analysis

These pillars use BA concepts to create content that is credible, practical, and specific to education workflows.

## Pillar 1: Schools Need Workflows, Not Prompts

BA foundation:

- Process Modeling
- Business Rules Analysis
- Acceptance Criteria

Core idea:

AI prompts create isolated outputs. Schools need repeatable workflows with review, approval, and export.

Example topics:

- Why schools need AI workflows, not AI prompts
- The draft-review-approve-export model for education AI
- How to turn a lesson planning process into an AI-assisted workflow
- Why academic managers need approval checkpoints, not just generated content

## Pillar 2: Traceability From Curriculum To Output

BA foundation:

- Requirements Traceability
- Data Quality
- Acceptance Criteria

Core idea:

Education AI should connect curriculum standards to generated materials and final exported files.

Example topics:

- From curriculum objective to quiz question: what traceability looks like in education AI
- Why lesson slides should connect back to assessment matrices
- How schools can check whether AI-generated content matches academic standards
- The missing link between curriculum, lesson plans, and assessment

## Pillar 3: Human Approval Is A Business Rule

BA foundation:

- Business Rules Analysis
- Risk Analysis
- Governance

Core idea:

Human review should not be an afterthought. It should be a required workflow rule.

Example topics:

- Why human approval is not optional in education AI
- The safest AI workflow for schools: draft, review, approve, export
- How schools can define approval rules for AI-generated lesson plans
- What academic managers should review before approving AI-generated materials

## Pillar 4: Teacher Workload Is A Process Problem

BA foundation:

- Root Cause Analysis
- Process Analysis
- Value Proposition Canvas

Core idea:

Teacher workload is not only about task volume. It is often caused by repeated manual document workflows.

Example topics:

- How to model teacher workload before automating it
- Why teachers spend too much time formatting academic documents
- The hidden workflow cost of lesson planning
- How schools can standardize documents without adding admin work

## Pillar 5: Privacy And Trust Are Requirements

BA foundation:

- Non-Functional Requirements
- Risk Analysis
- Stakeholder Analysis

Core idea:

Privacy, transparency, and quality are not marketing claims. They are requirements that should shape product design.

Example topics:

- Why OpenEdu stores progress locally
- Privacy-first learning as a product requirement
- What "no login" changes about student practice
- How to design education tools that collect less data

## Pillar 6: Content Quality Requires Feedback Loops

BA foundation:

- Data Quality
- Item Tracking
- Reviews
- Metrics And KPIs

Core idea:

Education content improves when errors, feedback, and usage signals are tracked and reviewed.

Example topics:

- A question bank is only as good as its review workflow
- How community error reporting improves OpenEdu
- What schools should track in AI-generated academic documents
- Why every education AI output needs a feedback loop
