## 2. LinkedIn Post

The safest education technology is not always the most complex.

Sometimes it is the product that collects less.

OpenEdu is a free practice platform for Vietnamese students from grade 1 to grade 12. It supports practice across Math, Vietnamese/Literature, Physics, Chemistry, and Informatics with 10,700+ questions aligned with GDPT 2018.

But the product decision that matters most is this:

No login is required.

OpenEdu does not need a student profile to let a student practice. Progress is stored locally on the browser, not sent to a central server.

This creates a clear trade-off: progress may not sync across every device.

But it also creates a clear benefit: students can practice without giving unnecessary personal data.

OpenEdu is not trying to replace teachers or parents. It provides structured practice while humans remain responsible for explanation, guidance, and learning support.

That is the kind of education technology worth building: useful, limited, transparent, and privacy-first.

CTA: Read the OpenEdu privacy principles.
