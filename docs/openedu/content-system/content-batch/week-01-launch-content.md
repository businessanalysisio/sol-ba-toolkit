## 1. Blog Article

# Why OpenEdu Does Not Ask Students To Log In

Most students do not open a practice platform because they want to create another account.

They open it because they want to review one topic, answer a few questions, prepare for a test, or check whether they understand the lesson.

That is why OpenEdu is designed without login.

OpenEdu is a free online practice platform for Vietnamese students from grade 1 to grade 12. It provides more than 10,700 questions aligned with the 2018 General Education Program across Math, Vietnamese/Literature, Physics, Chemistry, and Informatics.

The platform follows a simple principle:

**Practice first. No login. No personal data collection.**

## The Problem With Account-First Learning

Many online learning tools ask students to create an account before they can practice.

That process may include:

- email
- phone number
- full name
- school
- grade
- parent information
- password

For some products, accounts are necessary. But for quick practice, they often create friction.

A student who wants to practice fractions should not have to complete a registration flow first.

## Why Privacy Matters

Students are not ordinary users. They are children and young learners.

Educational platforms should be careful about what they collect, why they collect it, and whether collection is necessary at all.

OpenEdu takes a minimal approach:

- no login required
- no student profile required
- no email required
- no phone number required
- progress stored locally on the browser

This means students can practice without sending personal progress data to a central server.

## What OpenEdu Still Provides

No login does not mean no structure.

OpenEdu still provides:

- grade selection
- subject selection
- topic-based practice
- answers
- progress on the local device
- curriculum-aligned questions

The platform is designed to support self-study, not replace teachers or parents.

Teachers still explain. Parents still guide. Students still ask questions. OpenEdu simply makes practice easier to access.

## The Trade-Off

Local storage has one trade-off: progress may not automatically follow the student across devices.

OpenEdu accepts this trade-off because privacy is part of the product philosophy.

For a community practice platform, protecting students from unnecessary data collection matters more than centralized tracking.

## Final Thought

OpenEdu does not ask students to log in because the goal is not to build a user profile.

The goal is to help students practice.

Free practice should be simple, useful, and privacy-first.

**Open practice. No login. No personal data collection.**
