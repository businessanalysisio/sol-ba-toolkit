# Sofia Rewrites - OpenEdu Launch Post

## Version A: Sharper

Students should not need to create an account just to practice one topic.

OpenEdu is a free practice platform for Vietnamese students from grade 1 to grade 12.

It offers 10,700+ questions aligned with the 2018 General Education Program across Math, Vietnamese/Literature, Physics, Chemistry, and Informatics.

The principle is simple:

**Practice first. No login. No personal data collection.**

Students choose a grade, subject, and topic, then start practicing immediately.

Progress is stored locally on the browser, not sent to a central student profile.

OpenEdu does not replace teachers or parents. It supports practice while humans continue to teach, explain, and guide.

Free learning should not require unnecessary data collection.

Start practicing with one topic today.

## Version B: More Educational

Good practice should be easy to access.

When a student wants to review fractions, grammar, motion, chemical reactions, or algorithms, the first step should not be account registration. It should be learning.

OpenEdu is a free online practice platform for Vietnamese students from grade 1 to grade 12. It provides 10,700+ questions aligned with the 2018 General Education Program.

Students can select their grade, subject, and topic, then begin immediately.

No login is required.

No personal data is collected.

Learning progress is stored locally on the student's own browser.

This design protects privacy while keeping the learning experience simple. OpenEdu supports self-study, but it does not replace teachers, parents, or classroom explanation.

Its role is focused: provide reliable practice questions and remove unnecessary barriers.

## Version C: More Credible

OpenEdu is built on a practical education principle: students should be able to practice without surrendering personal data.

As part of the Agentra ecosystem, OpenEdu provides a free, login-free practice environment for Vietnamese students from grade 1 to grade 12.

The platform includes more than 10,700 questions aligned with the 2018 General Education Program, covering Math, Vietnamese/Literature, Physics, Chemistry, and Informatics.

OpenEdu separates technology from teaching responsibility.

The platform provides structured practice. Teachers and parents remain responsible for explanation, guidance, and learning support.

Student progress is stored locally in the browser. OpenEdu does not require accounts, does not collect personal profiles, and does not send learning progress to a central server.

This is open practice: accessible, privacy-first, and community-improvable.
