# Maya Research Brief - OpenEdu

## Research Theme

Free, privacy-first online practice for Vietnamese students from grades 1 to 12.

## Repeated Pain Points

1. Students want to practice quickly but many platforms require login.
2. Parents worry about children giving personal data to learning apps.
3. Teachers need reliable question sources aligned with GDPT 2018.
4. Online resources are inconsistent in quality and answer accuracy.
5. Students often need extra practice by topic, not a full paid course.
6. Families want free resources that are genuinely free.
7. Younger students need simple interfaces with fewer distractions.
8. Progress tracking can become invasive when platforms collect too much data.
9. Community correction is often missing from educational content.
10. Many tools position themselves as full learning replacements instead of support resources.

## Core Objections

| Objection | OpenEdu Response |
|---|---|
| Free platforms often collect data | OpenEdu does not require login and stores progress locally |
| Online questions may be wrong | OpenEdu uses structured content and community reporting |
| Students still need explanation | OpenEdu is practice support, not a teacher replacement |
| No account means no cloud sync | Correct; OpenEdu prioritizes privacy over centralized tracking |
| Free tools may become paid later | OpenEdu should communicate its community and open-practice commitment clearly |
