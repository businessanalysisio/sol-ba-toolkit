# Linh First Draft - Launch Post

## Draft

Students do not always need another learning account.

Sometimes they just need to practice one topic before class, review a weak area before a test, or answer a few questions after homework.

That is why OpenEdu is built around a simple idea:

**Open practice. No login. No personal data collection.**

OpenEdu is a free online practice platform for Vietnamese students from grade 1 to grade 12. It includes more than 10,700 questions aligned with the 2018 General Education Program across subjects such as Math, Vietnamese/Literature, Physics, Chemistry, and Informatics.

Students can choose a grade, choose a subject, choose a topic, and start practicing immediately.

No email.

No phone number.

No student profile on a server.

Progress is stored locally on the student's own browser. That means OpenEdu does not need to collect personal data just to help a student practice.

OpenEdu is not here to replace teachers or parents. It is here to support practice. Teachers still explain. Parents still guide. Students still learn through effort.

Technology should make practice easier, not more invasive.

Try OpenEdu and start with one topic today.
