# Olivia Brand Review - OpenEdu

## Review Result

Approved with minor positioning guidance.

## Brand Fit Score

9/10

## What Works

- Strong privacy-first positioning.
- Clear distinction between practice support and human teaching.
- Specific product proof: 10,700+ questions, GDPT 2018 alignment, no login.
- Avoids AI hype and avoids replacing teachers.
- Fits Agentra ecosystem without making OpenEdu sound commercial.

## Required Guardrails

- Do not claim every question is perfect. Use "verified", "community-reviewed", or "quality-controlled" carefully.
- Keep "no data collection" precise: no personal data collection; local progress may still exist on the user's device.
- Avoid saying OpenEdu replaces tutoring, classroom instruction, or parental guidance.
- Always include the privacy promise and open-practice philosophy.

## Approved Positioning Line

OpenEdu is a free, privacy-first practice platform for Vietnamese students: no login, no personal data collection, and curriculum-aligned questions for self-study support.
