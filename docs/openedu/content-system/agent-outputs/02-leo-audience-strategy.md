# Leo Audience Strategy - OpenEdu

## Primary Personas

### Student

- Job-to-be-done: Practice quickly by grade, subject, and topic.
- Main pain point: Too many steps before starting.
- Trigger: Upcoming quiz, test, homework, or weak topic.
- Message: "Vao hoc ngay, khong can dang nhap."
- CTA: Start practicing.

### Parent

- Job-to-be-done: Give children safe practice without sharing personal data.
- Main pain point: Concern about privacy and content quality.
- Trigger: Child needs extra practice at home.
- Message: "Con co the luyen tap ma khong can tao tai khoan."
- CTA: Try a topic with your child.

### Teacher

- Job-to-be-done: Find supplementary practice questions aligned with GDPT 2018.
- Main pain point: Online content quality is inconsistent.
- Trigger: Need quick practice set for revision.
- Message: "Kho cau hoi mo, co the bao loi va cai thien cung cong dong."
- CTA: Review questions or report an issue.

## Funnel Themes

| Stage | Theme | Content Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Privacy-first practice | Why no-login learning matters |
| Consideration | Content quality | How OpenEdu keeps questions aligned |
| Conversion | Immediate use | Choose class, subject, topic, and practice now |
