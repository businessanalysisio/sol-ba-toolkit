# OpenEdu Social Post Bank

## Post 1 - Parent Trust

Con muon luyen tap mot chu de, khong nhat thiet phai tao them mot tai khoan hoc tap.

OpenEdu giup hoc sinh chon lop, chon mon, chon chu de va lam bai ngay.

Khong dang nhap.  
Khong thu thap du lieu ca nhan.  
Tien do luu tren trinh duyet cua thiet bi.

OpenEdu khong thay the thay co hay cha me. Nen tang chi tao mot noi luyen tap mien phi, rieng tu va bam sat GDPT 2018.

CTA: Thu mot chu de hom nay.

## Post 2 - Teacher Support

OpenEdu khong phai la giao vien ao.

OpenEdu la kho luyen tap mo.

Giao vien van la nguoi giang giai, dinh huong va sua hieu lam. OpenEdu chi giup hoc sinh co them cau hoi de luyen theo lop, mon va chu de.

Neu cau hoi chua tot, cong dong co the bao loi va de xuat sua.

Do la cach mot kho cau hoi mo nen phat trien: co cau truc, co kiem chung, va co su tham gia cua giao vien.

## Post 3 - Privacy

Free learning should not require unnecessary data collection.

OpenEdu is built for students who need practice, not another user profile.

No login.  
No email.  
No phone number.  
Progress stored locally.

Open practice should be simple and privacy-first.

## Post 4 - Curriculum

OpenEdu co hon 10.700 cau hoi bam sat chuong trinh GDPT 2018.

Hoc sinh co the luyen theo:

- lop
- mon
- chu de

Muc tieu khong phai la thay the lop hoc. Muc tieu la giup hoc sinh co them noi luyen tap dang tin cay.

## Post 5 - Community Contribution

Mot kho cau hoi tot khong chi can so luong.

No can co che sua loi.

OpenEdu duoc xay dung nhu mot du an cong dong: giao vien, phu huynh va nguoi dung co the bao loi, de xuat chu de va dong gop cai thien noi dung.

Neu thay mot cau hoi chua ro, do khong phai la diem ket thuc. Do la diem bat dau cua qua trinh cai thien.
