# OpenEdu Whitepaper

## Nen tang luyen tap truc tuyen mo cho hoc sinh Viet Nam

### Mien phi that. Khong dang nhap. Khong thu thap du lieu ca nhan.

## 1. Executive Summary

OpenEdu la mot du an cong dong thuoc he sinh thai Agentra, cung cap nen tang luyen tap truc tuyen mien phi cho hoc sinh tu lop 1 den lop 12 tai Viet Nam.

Voi kho du lieu hon 10.700 cau hoi bam sat chuong trinh Giao duc pho thong 2018, OpenEdu giup hoc sinh co the tu luyen tap nhanh, don gian va rieng tu. Hoc sinh khong can tao tai khoan, khong can dang nhap, va khong can cung cap thong tin ca nhan.

OpenEdu duoc xay dung tren mot triet ly ro rang:

> Cong nghe chi nen ho tro viec luyen tap. Vai tro giang day, giai thich va dinh huong van thuoc ve thay co va cha me.

Tat ca tien do hoc tap cua hoc sinh duoc luu cuc bo tren trinh duyet ca nhan. Du lieu nay khong duoc gui len may chu, khong duoc dung de theo doi hanh vi, va khong duoc ban cho ben thu ba.

OpenEdu tap trung vao 4 gia tri cot loi:

- Mien phi that
- Khong thu thap du lieu ca nhan
- Noi dung bam sat GDPT 2018
- Dinh dang mo va co the kiem chung

## 2. Problem Space

### 2.1 Quyen rieng tu cua hoc sinh

Nhieu nen tang hoc tap truc tuyen yeu cau hoc sinh tao tai khoan, nhap email, so dien thoai, ten truong, lop hoc hoac thong tin phu huynh. Trong khi do, nhu cau cua hoc sinh trong nhieu tinh huong rat don gian: mo bai tap va luyen ngay.

Viec thu thap du lieu ca nhan tao ra nhieu van de:

- Hoc sinh bi yeu cau cung cap thong tin khong can thiet
- Phu huynh kho biet du lieu duoc luu va su dung nhu the nao
- Nha truong co them lo ngai ve bao mat va quyen rieng tu
- Nen tang hoc tap co the bien thanh cong cu theo doi hanh vi

OpenEdu chon cach tiep can nguoc lai: neu viec luyen tap co the dien ra ma khong can thu thap du lieu, thi khong nen thu thap.

### 2.2 Rao can tiep can

Dang ky tai khoan la mot rao can nho nhung co anh huong lon.

Hoc sinh muon luyen tap nhanh thuong gap cac buoc khong can thiet:

1. Tao tai khoan
2. Xac minh email
3. Chon goi hoc
4. Dien thong tin ca nhan
5. Dang nhap lai tren thiet bi khac

Moi buoc lam tang kha nang hoc sinh bo cuoc truoc khi bat dau hoc.

OpenEdu loai bo rao can nay. Hoc sinh chi can truy cap, chon lop, chon mon, chon chu de va bat dau luyen tap.

### 2.3 Do chinh xac cua noi dung

Mot van de lon cua tai lieu truc tuyen la do tin cay. Nhieu nguon bai tap:

- Khong bam sat chuong trinh GDPT 2018
- Khong ghi ro muc tieu bai hoc
- Co dap an sai hoac thieu giai thich
- Trinh bay khong dong nhat
- Khong co co che bao loi va chinh sua cong khai

Voi hoc sinh, noi dung sai co the tao hieu nham. Voi giao vien va phu huynh, noi dung khong kiem chung lam giam niem tin.

OpenEdu giai quyet van de nay bang kho cau hoi chuan hoa, co cau truc ro rang va cho phep cong dong bao loi, de xuat chinh sua va dong gop chu de moi.

## 3. Thesis

Triet ly cua OpenEdu la **luyen tap mo**.

OpenEdu tach bach vai tro cua cong nghe va con nguoi:

- Cong nghe cung cap kho bai tap, giao dien luyen tap va cong cu theo doi tien do cuc bo.
- Thay co va cha me giu vai tro giang day, giai thich, dong vien va dinh huong.

OpenEdu khong tu nhan la thay giao ao, khong thay the lop hoc, va khong bien viec hoc thanh mot he thong theo doi du lieu. Nen tang chi lam mot viec that tot: giup hoc sinh luyen tap de dang voi noi dung dang tin cay.

## 6. Privacy Model

Quyen rieng tu la mot phan cua thiet ke san pham, khong phai tinh nang phu.

OpenEdu ap dung cac nguyen tac:

- Khong yeu cau dang nhap
- Khong thu email
- Khong thu so dien thoai
- Khong tao ho so hoc sinh tren may chu
- Khong ban du lieu
- Khong theo doi tien do ca nhan tren he thong trung tam
- Tien do chi luu tren trinh duyet cua nguoi dung

Neu mot hoc sinh dung thiet bi khac, tien do co the khong dong bo. Day la mot trade-off co chu y: OpenEdu uu tien quyen rieng tu hon viec theo doi tap trung.

## 9. Relationship With Agentra

OpenEdu la du an cong dong trong he sinh thai Agentra.

Agentra tap trung vao workflow AI cho giao vien, nha truong va hoc lieu xuat ban. OpenEdu tap trung vao luyen tap mo cho hoc sinh.

Hai du an bo tro nhau:

- Agentra ho tro giao vien tao hoc lieu.
- OpenEdu giup hoc sinh luyen tap voi kho cau hoi mo.
- Agentra co the ho tro tao, chuan hoa va kiem duyet cau hoi.
- OpenEdu giu nguyen tac mien phi, rieng tu va cong dong.

## 10. Why OpenEdu Is Different

OpenEdu khac voi nhieu nen tang hoc tap truc tuyen o 5 diem:

1. **Khong dang nhap**  
   Hoc sinh co the luyen tap ngay.

2. **Khong thu thap du lieu ca nhan**  
   Nen tang khong can biet hoc sinh la ai de giup hoc sinh luyen tap.

3. **Noi dung bam sat GDPT 2018**  
   Cau hoi duoc to chuc theo chuong trinh va chu de.

4. **Tien do luu cuc bo**  
   Du lieu hoc tap nam tren thiet bi cua nguoi dung.

5. **Du an cong dong**  
   Giao vien, phu huynh va nguoi dung co the bao loi, dong gop va cai thien noi dung.

- Mau cau hoi chuan
- Dashboard loi noi dung
- Chu de can bo sung
- Cong khai danh sach nguoi dong gop neu ho dong y

- Bo de goi y theo chu de
- Trang huong dan phu huynh dong hanh
- Trang huong dan giao vien su dung OpenEdu nhu tai nguyen bo tro
- File xuat cau hoi o dinh dang mo

Trong khi nhieu san pham giao duc dang dua vao tai khoan, theo doi va dong bo du lieu, OpenEdu chon mot huong khac: toi gian, mo, rieng tu va bam sat noi dung hoc tap.
