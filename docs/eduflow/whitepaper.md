# EduFlow Whitepaper

## Cong cu soan bai thong minh cho giao vien STEM

### AI tao nhap. Giao vien kiem duyet. Hoc lieu xuat ban san sang su dung.

## 1. Executive Summary

EduFlow la nen tang ung dung AI nham ho tro giao vien STEM toi uu hoa quy trinh soan bai giang, thiet ke hoc lieu va tao cau hoi luyen tap theo dinh huong chuong trinh GDPT 2018.

Thay vi de giao vien phai soan tung slide, ve tung hinh minh hoa, viet tung cau hoi trac nghiem va kiem tra thu cong su dong bo giua muc tieu bai hoc, ma tran de va hoc lieu, EduFlow chuyen toan bo qua trinh nay thanh mot workflow co cau truc.

Giao vien bat dau bang viec chon mon hoc, lop hoc, chuong/bai va cau hinh ma tran danh gia theo cac muc do nhan thuc. Tu do, he thong AI tao ra bo hoc lieu dong bo gom:

- Slide bai giang
- Hinh anh minh hoa ky thuat cho Toan, Ly, Hoa, Tin
- Cau hoi trac nghiem kem dap an va loi giai
- Noi dung bam sat chuong trinh GDPT 2018
- Cau truc phu hop dinh huong Thong tu 22 va Cong van 5512

Diem cot loi cua EduFlow khong phai la thay the giao vien, ma la giam tai cac thao tac lap lai trong qua trinh soan giang. Giao vien van giu vai tro quyet dinh: kiem duyet noi dung, dieu chinh tinh su pham, xac nhan do chinh xac va phe duyet hoc lieu truoc khi su dung.

**EduFlow principle:** AI drafts. Teachers approve. Materials export ready to use.

## 2. Problem Space

### 2.1 Ap luc soan giang ngay cang lon

Giao vien STEM thuong phai xu ly nhieu loai cong viec trong mot bai giang:

- Xay dung muc tieu bai hoc
- Thiet ke tien trinh day hoc
- Soan slide
- Ve hinh minh hoa, do thi, so do luc, cau truc phan tu hoac thuat toan
- Viet cau hoi trac nghiem
- Tao dap an va loi giai
- Dam bao noi dung bam sat chuong trinh
- Dieu chinh hoc lieu theo nang luc hoc sinh

Nhung cong viec nay tieu ton nhieu thoi gian, dac biet voi nhung mon co yeu to ky thuat cao nhu Toan, Vat ly, Hoa hoc va Tin hoc.

### 2.2 Thieu su dong bo giua bai giang va danh gia

Trong thuc te, slide bai giang, hinh anh minh hoa va cau hoi kiem tra thuong duoc tao rieng le. Dieu nay dan den tinh trang:

- Slide khong bam sat ma tran de
- Quiz khong phan anh dung muc tieu bai hoc
- Cau hoi lech muc do nhan thuc
- Hoc lieu thieu tinh nhat quan giua cac giao vien
- Kho kiem tra chat luong noi dung theo chuan chung

Khi hoc lieu khong duoc tao tu cung mot cau truc dau vao, giao vien va to chuyen mon phai mat them thoi gian ra soat, chinh sua va chuan hoa.

### 2.3 Rao can ky thuat trong viec tao hoc lieu STEM

Các mon STEM yeu cau nhieu hinh anh chinh xac:

- Do thi ham so
- Hinh hoc phang va hinh hoc khong gian
- So do luc
- Mach dien
- Phuong trinh hoa hoc
- Mo hinh phan tu
- Luu do thuat toan
- Bieu do du lieu

Viec tao cac hinh anh nay bang cong cu van phong pho thong thuong gap loi:

- Vo hinh khi phong to
- Sai ty le
- Lech ky hieu
- Kho chinh sua
- Khong dong nhat giua slide va tai lieu
- Mat nhieu thoi gian de xuat ban dung dinh dang

EduFlow giai quyet van de nay bang cach tao hinh ky thuat bang ma hoac cau truc vector, giup hinh anh ro net, nhat quan va de tai su dung.

## 3. Thesis

EduFlow thay doi tu duy soan bai tu **thu cong tung phan** sang **workflow hoc lieu dong bo**.

Thay vi giao vien phai tao tung thanh phan rieng le, EduFlow su dung ma tran danh gia lam diem khoi dau. Tu ma tran do, he thong sinh ra bai giang, hinh minh hoa va quiz theo cung mot logic noi dung.

Mo hinh nay giup dam bao:

- Bai giang bam sat muc tieu hoc tap
- Cau hoi bam sat muc do nhan thuc
- Hinh anh minh hoa phu hop voi noi dung slide
- Hoc lieu co the kiem duyet truoc khi dung
- File dau ra san sang xuat ban duoi dang PowerPoint, Word hoac PDF

Giao vien khong bi loai khoi quy trinh. Nguoc lai, giao vien duoc dat vao vai tro quan trong hon: nguoi thiet ke muc tieu, kiem duyet chat luong va quyet dinh hoc lieu cuoi cung.

## 4. What Is EduFlow?

EduFlow la he thong tu dong hoa soan bai giang STEM dua tren AI, ma tran danh gia va quy trinh kiem duyet cua giao vien.

He thong hoat dong qua 3 buoc chinh.

## 5. Core Workflow

### Buoc 1: Chon nguon hoc lieu

Giao vien chon:

- Mon hoc: Toan, Vat ly, Hoa hoc, Tin hoc
- Khoi lop: 1-12
- Chuong hoac bai hoc
- Chu de kien thuc
- Chuan dau ra hoac yeu cau can dat

Muc tieu cua buoc nay la xac dinh ro pham vi bai hoc truoc khi he thong tao noi dung.

### Buoc 2: Lap ma tran danh gia

Giao vien cau hinh ma tran theo cac muc do nhan thuc.

| Muc do | Muc tieu |
|---|---|
| Nhan biet | Kiem tra kien thuc co ban |
| Thong hieu | Kiem tra kha nang giai thich va dien giai |
| Van dung | Kiem tra kha nang ap dung vao bai tap |
| Van dung cao | Kiem tra kha nang phan tich, tong hop, giai quyet van de |

Tu ma tran nay, EduFlow hieu duoc bai hoc can nhan manh noi dung nao, cau hoi nao can tao, va muc do kho can phan bo ra sao.

### Buoc 3: Workflow tu dong tao hoc lieu

Sau khi co nguon hoc lieu va ma tran, EduFlow tao bo san pham dong bo.

| Thanh phan | Mo ta |
|---|---|
| Slide bai giang | Cau truc bai hoc, muc tieu, noi dung chinh, vi du, hoat dong |
| Hinh ky thuat | Do thi, so do, cong thuc, mo hinh, bieu do duoc tao bang ma |
| Quiz luyen tap | Cau hoi trac nghiem theo ma tran, co dap an va loi giai |
| Tai lieu xuat ban | File PowerPoint, Word hoac PDF san sang su dung |

## 6. Human Review Layer

EduFlow khong van hanh theo mo hinh "AI tao xong la dung ngay".

He thong duoc thiet ke theo nguyen tac:

**AI tao nhap. Giao vien kiem duyet. Hoc lieu xuat ban san sang su dung.**

Giao vien kiem duyet:

- Tinh chinh xac chuyen mon
- Tinh phu hop voi trinh do hoc sinh
- Tinh su pham
- Muc do bam sat chuong trinh
- Cach dien dat
- Dap an va loi giai
- Hinh anh minh hoa
- Cau truc slide

Dieu nay giup EduFlow phu hop voi moi truong giao duc, noi chat luong hoc thuat va trach nhiem chuyen mon luon can con nguoi kiem soat.

- Chuan dau ra mon hoc

## 8. Key Use Cases

### Use Case 1: Tao bai giang Toan co do thi

Giao vien chon chu de ham so bac hai. EduFlow tao:

- Slide ly thuyet
- Do thi parabol ro net
- Vi du minh hoa
- Bai tap nhan biet, thong hieu, van dung
- Quiz kem dap an

### Use Case 2: Tao bai giang Vat ly co so do luc

Giao vien chon bai ve luc ma sat. EduFlow tao:

- Slide khai niem
- So do luc bang vector
- Vi du bai toan
- Cau hoi trac nghiem theo muc do
- Loi giai chi tiet

### Use Case 3: Tao bai giang Hoa hoc co phuong trinh phan ung

Giao vien chon bai phan ung oxi hoa khu. EduFlow tao:

- Slide noi dung
- Phuong trinh hoa hoc chuan dinh dang
- Bang so sanh chat oxi hoa va chat khu
- Quiz phan loai muc do
- File xuat ban PDF hoac PowerPoint

### Use Case 4: Tao bai giang Tin hoc co luu do thuat toan

Giao vien chon bai vong lap. EduFlow tao:

- Slide giai thich khai niem
- Luu do thuat toan
- Vi du ma gia
- Cau hoi kiem tra logic
- Bai tap van dung

## 10. Why EduFlow Is Different

EduFlow khong chi la cong cu hoi dap AI.

EduFlow khac biet o 5 diem:

1. **Bat dau tu ma tran danh gia**  
   Noi dung duoc tao theo cau truc kiem tra va muc tieu hoc tap.

2. **Tap trung vao STEM**  
   Ho tro hinh anh ky thuat, cong thuc, do thi va bai tap chuyen mon.

3. **Tao bo hoc lieu dong bo**  
   Slide, hinh anh va quiz duoc tao tu cung mot logic noi dung.

4. **Co lop kiem duyet cua giao vien**  
   Giao vien giu quyen quyet dinh cuoi cung.

5. **Xuat file san sang su dung**  
   Hoc lieu khong dung o van ban nhap ma co the xuat thanh Word, PDF, PowerPoint.

## 11. Implementation Roadmap

### Phase 1: MVP

Muc tieu: chung minh workflow tao hoc lieu STEM tu ma tran.

Tinh nang chinh:

- Chon mon, lop, chuong
- Tao ma tran cau hoi
- Sinh slide co ban
- Sinh quiz co dap an
- Tao hinh ky thuat don gian
- Xuat PDF/PPTX

### Phase 2: Teacher Review Workflow

Muc tieu: tang chat luong va kha nang kiem duyet.

Tinh nang chinh:

- Giao dien duyet tung slide
- Chinh sua cau hoi
- Kiem tra dap an
- Ghi chu phan hoi
- Phien ban truoc/sau chinh sua
- Xuat file sau phe duyet

### Phase 3: School-Level Standardization

Muc tieu: phuc vu to chuyen mon va nha truong.

Tinh nang chinh:

- Template theo truong
- Thu vien hoc lieu dung chung
- Phan quyen giao vien/to truong/quan ly
- Chuan hoa format
- Dashboard theo mon/lop/chuong
- Bao cao su dung

### Phase 4: Advanced STEM Engine

Muc tieu: nang cao chat luong hinh anh va bai tap.

Tinh nang chinh:

- Sinh do thi bang ma
- Sinh so do luc
- Sinh mo hinh hoa hoc
- Sinh luu do thuat toan
- Kiem tra do kho cau hoi
- Goi y cai thien bai giang

EduFlow giai quyet van de nay bang mot workflow ro rang: chon nguon hoc lieu, lap ma tran, tao bo hoc lieu dong bo, giao vien kiem duyet va xuat file san sang su dung.
